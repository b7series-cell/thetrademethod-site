When does 24-hour stock trading start?

Sunday, Dec 6, 2026 at 9 PM ET. From then on, US stocks trade from Sunday 9 PM to Friday 8 PM, with a one-hour pause from 8 to 9 PM each weeknight. The new part is an overnight session from 9 PM to 4 AM on Nasdaq, NYSE Arca, Cboe EDGX, MEMX and 24X.

What changes, and when

Four things to know before the first overnight session. All times are Eastern.

when

Starts Sunday, Dec 6 at 9 PM

Stocks will trade from Sunday 9 PM to Friday 8 PM, with a one-hour pause from 8 to 9 PM each weeknight.

what's new

An overnight session, 9 PM to 4 AM

On Nasdaq, NYSE Arca, Cboe EDGX, MEMX and 24X.

what stays

The day you know doesn't change

Premarket (4:00–9:30), regular hours (9:30–4:00) and after-hours (4:00–8:00) stay the same. So do the 9:30 open and 4:00 close auctions and the official close.

how it counts

Overnight counts toward the next day

Trades between 9 PM and midnight count toward the next day, the way futures work. Overnight trades add to volume but don't set the day's last price, high or low.

Overnight is only about 1% of share volume.

What it means for your levels

The setups on this site are built on premarket and regular-hours levels. Those hours don't move, and overnight trades don't set the day's last price, high or low.

Stays the same

  • Premarket runs 4:00 to 9:30, so the premarket high and low come from the same window.
  • The 9:30 open and the 4:00 close auctions, and the official close.
  • The day's last price, high and low, which overnight trades don't set.
  • After-hours, 4:00 to 8:00.

Worth knowing

  • A trade at 10 PM Monday counts toward Tuesday, the way futures work.
  • Overnight trades add to volume, but it's only about 1% of share volume (0.9% in August 2026, per SEC staff data), so overnight trading will be thin.
  • There are no automatic trading pauses overnight. Each stock gets fixed price bands, 20% beyond its reference prices, and trades outside them are blocked. A stock the listing exchange halts overnight stays halted until 4 AM.
  • Clearing doesn't change: NSCC clears overnight trades the same night, and they settle T+1 from the next-day trade date. A 10 PM Monday buy settles Wednesday.
  • Check if your broker and your charts offer the overnight session, and how your chart's extended-hours setting shows it. The blog post How does 23-hour stock trading work? lists brokers with overnight trading and how much volume to expect.

Example: news hits at 11 PM Monday. A stock can now react in the overnight session on Nasdaq, NYSE Arca, Cboe EDGX, MEMX or 24X, and those trades count toward Tuesday. Tuesday's open is still the 9:30 auction, and Tuesday's high and low still come from the day you know.

Questions

Do premarket and regular hours change?

No. Premarket (4:00 to 9:30), regular hours (9:30 to 4:00) and after-hours (4:00 to 8:00) stay the same, and so do the 9:30 open and 4:00 close auctions and the official close.

Which day does an overnight trade count toward?

The next one. Trades between 9 PM and midnight count toward the next day, the way futures work.

Do overnight trades change the day's high or low?

No. Overnight trades add to volume but don't set the day's last price, high or low.

How much trading happens overnight?

Only about 1% of share volume (0.9% in August 2026, per SEC staff data), so the overnight session is thin.

Are there trading halts overnight?

Not automatic ones. Each stock gets fixed overnight price bands, 20% beyond its reference prices, and trades outside them are blocked with no 5-minute pause. The listing exchange can still halt a stock, and it then stays halted until 4 AM.

How are overnight trades cleared?

The same way as daytime trades. NSCC, part of DTCC, clears them 24x5, and they settle T+1 from the next-day trade date, so a 10 PM Monday buy settles Wednesday.
