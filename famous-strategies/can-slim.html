CAN SLIM

What did the biggest stock winners have in common before their big moves? O'Neil's answer is seven letters: strong earnings, something new, heavy buying, market leadership and a rising market. Buy the breakout, and cut every loss at 7 to 8%. Buy leaders, cut losers fast.

What is CAN SLIM?

CAN SLIM is William O'Neil's growth stock method from How to Make Money in Stocks (1988). Each letter is a test a stock must pass before its big move: strong current and annual earnings, something new, supply and demand, a market leader, institutional sponsorship, and a market in a confirmed uptrend. You buy the breakout from a sound base on heavy volume and cut every loss at 7 to 8%.

Seven letters

O'Neil studied the biggest winners in the US market and listed what they had in common just before their big moves. CAN SLIM is that list, turned into a checklist a stock has to pass before you look at its chart.

The checklist

1

The rule

  • M first: only buy when the general market is in a confirmed uptrend.
  • Screen for C, A, N, S, L and I: strong and accelerating earnings, something new, real demand, a leading stock in a leading group, and growing ownership by good funds.
  • Buy when a stock that passes breaks out of a sound base on heavy volume, within 5% of the pivot. Sell any position that falls 7 to 8% below your cost, and take most gains at 20 to 25%.

It mixes fundamentals and charts on purpose: the earnings pick the stock, the chart picks the day, and the market picks whether you're buying at all.

Growth, not value

CAN SLIM buys the opposite of what a value screen looks for.

CAN SLIMExpensive and rising

High earnings growth, near new highs, strong relative strength. O'Neil: buy stocks that are going up, not bargains.

value strategiesCheap and ignored

Low prices against earnings or book value, often near lows. See the Magic Formula and Value and Size pages.

The buy point

A stock that passes the checklist still has to be bought at the right moment. O'Neil's moment is the breakout from a base, and his favorite base is the cup with handle.

Cup, handle, breakout

2

Buying the breakout

  • Wait for a base of at least seven weeks: a cup with handle, a double bottom or a flat base.
  • The pivot is the top of the handle (or the base's high). Buy as the stock trades through it.
  • Breakout volume should be at least 40 to 50% above the stock's average. Don't buy more than 5% above the pivot.

Buying near the pivot keeps the 7 to 8% stop below the base. Chase it 10% higher and a normal pullback stops you out.

Buy high, sell higher. O'Neil's most repeated, and most resisted, idea: the best stocks look expensive when they're ready to move, and the ones near their lows usually have a reason to be there.

Selling

O'Neil spent as much of the book on selling as on buying. The loss rule is the part of CAN SLIM that has no exceptions.

Small losses, bigger gains

always

Cut at 7–8%

Sell any stock that falls 7 to 8% below what you paid. No averaging down, no waiting for it to come back.

usually

Take 20–25%

Take most gains when a stock is up 20 to 25%. With the loss rule, that's about 3 to 1.

the exception

Hold the monsters

A stock that gains 20% within three weeks of breaking out is held at least eight weeks.

the math

Why it works

Win only a third of the time at +20% and lose at −7%, and you still come out ahead.

M: the market decides

The last letter overrides the other six. O'Neil read the market's own chart for when to step aside and when to start buying again.

The strategy, step by step

The whole thing in five steps. Click through them.

1

Market

  • Is the general market in a confirmed uptrend? If not, don't buy.
  • Count distribution days; wait for a follow-through day after a correction.

Three out of four stocks follow the market.

2

Screen

  • C and A: quarterly and annual earnings up 25%+, ROE 17%+.
  • N, S, L, I: something new, real demand, relative strength 80+, more good funds buying.

The checklist makes a stock a candidate.

3

Base

  • A base of at least seven weeks: a cup with handle, double bottom or flat base.
  • Mark the pivot: the top of the handle.

The base is where weak holders leave.

4

Buy

  • Through the pivot on volume 40–50%+ above average.
  • No more than 5% above the pivot.

Close to the pivot keeps the stop below the base.

5

Sell

  • Always at 7–8% below your cost.
  • Most gains at 20–25%, unless it gained 20% in three weeks (then hold eight).

The loss rule is the one with no exceptions.

We tested it Slightly better than random

A little better than random entries

The price and volume rules on the whole market made +1.3% a trade. Random entries on the same days with the same exits made +0.4 to +1.1%.

  • 2,474trades, price and volume rules
  • +1.3%a trade
  • +0.4 to +1.1%random entries
  • +1.7%with the earnings letters (140 trades)
  • Adding C, A and ROE of 17% or more from SEC filings lifted it to +1.7% a trade, but on only 140 trades.

How we tested. Every US stock that traded from Nov 2021 to Nov 2026, delisted ones included, so there's no survivorship bias. Company figures from SEC filings, used only from the day they were filed. 0.5 bp a side on SPY, QQQ and ETFs, 5 bp a side on single stocks. Stock returns are price only; ETF returns include dividends. Five years is short for a monthly strategy, and 2022 to 2026 was a strong large-cap market that is hard for any timing rule to beat. +1R means a trade won what it risked. The indicator

One TradingView script for this strategy (Pine Script v6). It checks six of the seven letters from TradingView's earnings data and the chart, marks the pivot, the 5% buy zone and an 8% stop, and alerts on a breakout that passes. It marks and alerts. It doesn't place trades.

  • CAN SLIM Checklist

    alerts

    A checklist table on your chart: C (quarterly EPS vs a year ago), A (three-year EPS growth and return on equity), N (within 15% of the 52-week high), S (volume vs the 50-day average), L (12-month return vs SPY) and M (SPY above its 50- and 200-day averages). I, institutional ownership, isn't in TradingView's data, so it says to check by hand.

    On the chart: the pivot (the high of the last seven weeks), the top of the buy zone 5% above it, and a breakout label with the score when price closes through the pivot on heavy volume in an uptrending market. The 8% stop is drawn from the entry, with a label if it's hit.

    L here is the stock against SPY, a simple stand-in for IBD's relative strength rating, which ranks every stock. Every threshold is a setting. Use a daily chart of a stock that reports earnings.

    Download source (.pine)

Add it to TradingView

  1. Copy the code below, or download the .pine file.
  2. In TradingView, open the Pine Editor at the bottom of a chart, paste it in, and click Add to chart.
  3. Use a daily chart of a growth stock. Check the I (institutions) row yourself in the stock's fund-ownership data.
  4. To get alerted, create an alert on the chart and pick CAN SLIM breakout or Stop hit.

//@version=6
// =====================================================================
// CAN SLIM Checklist — thetrademethod.com
// https://thetrademethod.com/famous-strategies/can-slim.html
//
// William O'Neil, How to Make Money in Stocks (1988): buy growth leaders
// breaking out of a sound base, in a rising market, and cut every loss
// at 7-8%.
//
//   C  Latest quarter's EPS up at least 25% from the same quarter a year ago
//   A  Annual EPS growth of 25%+ over 3 years, and return on equity 17%+
//   N  Price near a new high (within 15% of the 52-week high)
//   S  Breakout volume at least 40% above the 50-day average
//   L  Relative strength: beating the benchmark over 12 months
//   I  Institutional sponsorship: not in TradingView data, check by hand
//   M  Market in an uptrend: the benchmark above its 50- and 200-day averages
//
// It also draws the pivot (the high of the base), the buy zone up to 5%
// above it, and a stop 8% under the entry. Daily chart, stocks only.
// Marks and alerts only; it places no orders.
// =====================================================================
indicator("CAN SLIM Checklist", shorttitle = "CAN SLIM", overlay = true, max_lines_count = 100, max_labels_count = 100)

grpF = "Fundamentals"
cMin   = input.float(25, "C: quarterly EPS growth at least (%)", group = grpF)
aMin   = input.float(25, "A: annual EPS growth at least (%/yr, 3 years)", group = grpF)
roeMin = input.float(17, "A: return on equity at least (%)", group = grpF)
grpT = "Chart"
baseLen = input.int(35, "Base length for the pivot (bars, 35 = 7 weeks)", minval = 10, group = grpT)
volMult = input.float(1.4, "S: breakout volume vs 50-day average", step = 0.1, group = grpT)
nearHi  = input.float(15, "N: within this % of the 52-week high", group = grpT)
stopPct = input.float(8, "Stop under entry (%)", step = 0.5, group = grpT)
zonePct = input.float(5, "Buy zone above pivot (%)", step = 0.5, group = grpT)
bench   = input.symbol("AMEX:SPY", "Benchmark (for L and M)", group = grpT)
minScore = input.int(5, "Signal only if at least this many letters pass (of 6 checked)", minval = 0, maxval = 6, group = grpT)

// ---------- C and A ----------
epsQ = request.financial(syminfo.tickerid, "EARNINGS_PER_SHARE_DILUTED", "FQ", ignore_invalid_symbol = true)
epsY = request.financial(syminfo.tickerid, "EARNINGS_PER_SHARE_DILUTED", "FY", ignore_invalid_symbol = true)
roe  = request.financial(syminfo.tickerid, "RETURN_ON_EQUITY", "FY", ignore_invalid_symbol = true)
var qs = array.new<float>()
var ys = array.new<float>()
if not na(epsQ) and (array.size(qs) == 0 or epsQ != array.get(qs, array.size(qs) - 1))
    array.push(qs, epsQ)
if not na(epsY) and (array.size(ys) == 0 or epsY != array.get(ys, array.size(ys) - 1))
    array.push(ys, epsY)
nq = array.size(qs)
ny = array.size(ys)
qNow  = nq >= 1 ? array.get(qs, nq - 1) : na
qAgo  = nq >= 5 ? array.get(qs, nq - 5) : na
cGrow = not na(qAgo) and qAgo > 0 ? (qNow / qAgo - 1) * 100 : na
yNow  = ny >= 1 ? array.get(ys, ny - 1) : na
y3    = ny >= 4 ? array.get(ys, ny - 4) : na
aGrow = not na(y3) and y3 > 0 and yNow > 0 ? (math.pow(yNow / y3, 1.0 / 3) - 1) * 100 : na
passC = not na(cGrow) and cGrow >= cMin
passA = not na(aGrow) and aGrow >= aMin and not na(roe) and roe >= roeMin

// ---------- N, S, L, M ----------
hi52  = ta.highest(high, 252)
passN = close >= hi52 * (1 - nearHi / 100)
avgV  = ta.sma(volume, 50)
pivot = ta.highest(high, baseLen)[1]
brk   = close > pivot and close[1] <= pivot[1]
passS = volume >= avgV * volMult
bC    = request.security(bench, "D", close, ignore_invalid_symbol = true)
rs    = (close / close[252] - 1) - (bC / bC[252] - 1)
passL = not na(rs) and rs > 0
bMa50  = request.security(bench, "D", ta.sma(close, 50), ignore_invalid_symbol = true)
bMa200 = request.security(bench, "D", ta.sma(close, 200), ignore_invalid_symbol = true)
passM = bC > bMa50 and bC > bMa200

score = (passC ? 1 : 0) + (passA ? 1 : 0) + (passN ? 1 : 0) + (passS ? 1 : 0) + (passL ? 1 : 0) + (passM ? 1 : 0)
signal = brk and passS and passM and score >= minScore

// ---------- trade levels ----------
var float entry = na
var float stopL = na
var line pivL = na
var line zoneL = na
var line stopLn = na
if signal
    entry := close
    stopL := close * (1 - stopPct / 100)
    pivL := line.new(bar_index, pivot, bar_index + 25, pivot, color = color.new(#2997ff, 0), width = 2)
    zoneL := line.new(bar_index, pivot * (1 + zonePct / 100), bar_index + 25, pivot * (1 + zonePct / 100), color = color.new(#f4b942, 0), style = line.style_dashed)
    stopLn := line.new(bar_index, stopL, bar_index + 25, stopL, color = color.new(#ff453a, 0), style = line.style_dashed)
    label.new(bar_index, low, "CAN SLIM breakout\n" + str.tostring(score) + "/6 + check I", style = label.style_label_up, color = color.new(#30d158, 10), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
stopHit = not na(stopL) and close < stopL
if stopHit
    label.new(bar_index, high, "-" + str.tostring(stopPct) + "% stop", style = label.style_label_down, color = color.new(#ff453a, 10), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
    entry := na
    stopL := na

plot(pivot, "Pivot (base high)", color = color.new(#2997ff, 60), style = plot.style_stepline)
plot(pivot * (1 + zonePct / 100), "Buy zone top", color = color.new(#f4b942, 70), style = plot.style_stepline)
plot(stopL, "Stop", color = color.new(#ff453a, 0), style = plot.style_linebr)
barcolor(brk and passS ? #30d158 : na, title = "Breakout on volume")

alertcondition(signal, "CAN SLIM breakout", "{{ticker}}: breakout above the pivot on volume, market in an uptrend")
alertcondition(stopHit, "Stop hit", "{{ticker}}: closed below the 8% stop")

var table tb = table.new(position.top_right, 3, 8, bgcolor = color.new(#1d1d1f, 0), border_width = 1, border_color = color.new(#2c2c2e, 0))
cell(int r, string k, string what, bool ok, bool known) =>
    table.cell(tb, 0, r, k, text_color = color.new(#f5f5f7, 0), text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 1, r, what, text_color = color.new(#86868b, 0), text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 2, r, not known ? "?" : ok ? "✓" : "✗", text_color = not known ? color.new(#f4b942, 0) : ok ? color.new(#30d158, 0) : color.new(#ff453a, 0), text_size = size.small)
if barstate.islast
    cell(0, "C", na(cGrow) ? "EPS q/q-4: n/a" : "EPS vs yr ago " + str.tostring(cGrow, "#") + "%", passC, not na(cGrow))
    cell(1, "A", na(aGrow) ? "3-yr EPS: n/a" : "3-yr EPS " + str.tostring(aGrow, "#") + "%/yr, ROE " + str.tostring(roe, "#") + "%", passA, not na(aGrow))
    cell(2, "N", "within " + str.tostring(nearHi, "#") + "% of 52-wk high", passN, true)
    cell(3, "S", "volume " + str.tostring(volume / avgV, "#.0") + "x avg", passS, true)
    cell(4, "L", na(rs) ? "12-mo vs bench: n/a" : "12-mo vs bench " + str.tostring(rs * 100, "#") + " pts", passL, not na(rs))
    cell(5, "I", "institutions: check by hand", false, false)
    cell(6, "M", "benchmark above 50 & 200 MA", passM, true)
    table.cell(tb, 0, 7, "Score", text_color = color.new(#86868b, 0), text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 1, 7, str.tostring(score) + " of 6 checked", text_color = color.new(#f5f5f7, 0), text_size = size.small)

Questions

What earnings does CAN SLIM look for?

Quarterly and annual earnings per share growth of 25% or more, with return on equity of 17% or more.

When do you buy a CAN SLIM stock?

Only when the general market is in a confirmed uptrend, on a breakout over the pivot of a sound base with volume 40 to 50% or more above normal, within 5% of the pivot.

When do you sell?

Always on a loss of 7 to 8% from your cost. Most gains are taken at 20 to 25%.

Does CAN SLIM work?

A little better than random in our test. The price and volume rules on the whole US market made +1.3% a trade over 2,474 trades, against +0.4 to +1.1% for random entries with the same exits. Adding the earnings letters lifted it to +1.7%, on only 140 trades.

Try it on paper first.

Find five stocks that pass the checklist and are building bases. Write down each pivot, the 5% buy zone and the 8% stop before anything happens. Track every breakout for three months, and count how many hit the stop before 20%.

  • Check M before every buy, not just once.
  • Never move the stop lower.
  • Note how many breakouts failed in a weak market.
