Intraday Momentum

Tested: No edge

On the S&P 500, the two ends of the trading day are linked. The return from yesterday's close to 10:00 am tends to point the way the last half-hour goes. Trade 30 minutes a day, in that direction. The open points, the close follows.

Two ends of the day

The authors cut every SPY trading day into thirteen half-hours and asked which ones predict which. Only one link stood out: the first half-hour and the last.

The first and the last

1

The rule

  • Measure the first half-hour return: from yesterday's 4:00 pm close to 10:00 am today, overnight gap included.
  • At 3:30 pm, buy SPY if that return was positive, or sell it short if it was negative.
  • Close the position at the 4:00 pm close. One 30-minute trade a day, never held overnight.

Big traders who rebalance once a day, and traders who get information late, both tend to act near the close in the direction the day's news pointed in the morning.

Why the close

The paper's two explanations.

rebalancingInfrequent trades

Some funds and hedgers rebalance once a day, near the close, in the direction the market has moved.

late informationLate-informed traders

Traders who react slowly to the morning's news push the price the same way in the last half-hour.

Trading it

There is no chart reading. The first half-hour sets the side, the clock sets the entry and the exit.

Two days, two trades

A small edge

The pattern is real in the data, and it's weak on any single day. That matters for how it's traded.

Mostly noise, slightly tilted

2

When it was stronger

  • On more volatile days and higher-volume days.
  • In recessions, and on days with major economic news releases.
  • It showed up in ten other heavily traded US and international ETFs, not just SPY.

Those are the days when more information arrives in the morning, and more of it is still being acted on at the close.

Repeated daily, small edges add up. An R-squared of 1.6% means the first half-hour explains very little of any one afternoon. The paper's result comes from taking the trade on every one of about 5,000 days.

The strategy, step by step

The whole thing in five steps. Click through them.

1

Measure

  • At 10:00 am, compare SPY with yesterday's 4:00 pm close.
  • Up or down: that's the side for today.

The overnight gap counts.

2

Wait

  • Do nothing for the rest of the day.
  • The middle half-hours showed no pattern.

The trade is at the other end.

3

Enter

  • At 3:30 pm: long if the first half-hour was up, short if down.
  • Optional: require 3:00–3:30 pm to agree.

Thirty minutes of exposure.

4

Exit

  • Close at 4:00 pm.
  • Never hold overnight.

The edge is only the last half-hour.

5

Costs

  • Use the most liquid fund and the cheapest execution you have.
  • Track your fills against the 3:30 and 4:00 prices.

A 30-minute edge is easily eaten by costs.

We tested it No edge

The first half hour didn't predict the last

Trading the last half hour in the direction of the morning was right on 49% of days, and slightly negative overall.

  • 49%of days right
  • −0.01%a day, SPY
  • −0.02%a day, QQQ

How we tested. Every US stock that traded from Nov 2021 to Nov 2026, delisted ones included, so there's no survivorship bias. Company figures from SEC filings, used only from the day they were filed. 0.5 bp a side on SPY, QQQ and ETFs, 5 bp a side on single stocks. Stock returns are price only; ETF returns include dividends. Five years is short for a monthly strategy, and 2022 to 2026 was a strong large-cap market that is hard for any timing rule to beat. +1R means a trade won what it risked. The indicator

One TradingView script for this strategy (Pine Script v6). It measures the first half-hour from yesterday's close, marks the 3:30 pm long or short, labels each day's result at the close, and keeps a running hit rate. It marks and alerts. It doesn't place trades.

  • Intraday Momentum

    alerts

    On the chart: the first and last half-hours shaded, a long or short label at 3:30 pm with the first half-hour's return, and a label at the close with the last half-hour's result in the trade's direction.

    A table with today's first half-hour, the 3:30 call, and for every day on the chart: the number of days, the share called right and the average result. An option requires the 3:00–3:30 pm half-hour to agree, which the paper found adds to the prediction.

    On the last 464 days of SPY 30-minute bars, our run called 47.8% right, with an average near zero, before costs. The paper's 54.37% covers 1993–2013. Check it on your own data before trading it.

Add it to TradingView

  1. Copy the code below, or download the .pine file.
  2. In TradingView, open the Pine Editor at the bottom of a chart, paste it in, and click Add to chart.

  4. To get alerted, create an alert on the chart and pick Intraday momentum: long or Intraday momentum: short.

//@version=6
// =====================================================================
// Intraday Momentum (first half-hour -> last half-hour) — thetrademethod.com
// https://thetrademethod.com/famous-strategies/intraday-momentum.html
//
// Gao, Han, Li & Zhou, Market Intraday Momentum (Journal of Financial
// Economics, 2018), on SPY 1993-2013:
//
//   r1   = return from yesterday's 4:00 pm close to 10:00 am today
//   Rule = at 3:30 pm, go long if r1 > 0, short if r1 < 0;
//          close at the 4:00 pm close
//
// Optional: also require the 12th half-hour (3:00-3:30 pm) to point the
// same way; the paper found it adds to the prediction.
// Use a 1-, 5-, 15- or 30-minute chart of SPY, regular hours only.
// Marks and alerts only; it places no orders.
// =====================================================================
indicator("Intraday Momentum", shorttitle = "IntradayMom", overlay = true, max_labels_count = 300)

useR12 = input.bool(false, "Also require 3:00-3:30 pm to agree")
tz = "America/New_York"

hC = hour(time_close, tz)
mC = minute(time_close, tz)
hO = hour(time, tz)
mO = minute(time, tz)

var float prevClose = na
var float p10 = na
var float p15 = na
var float r1 = na
var float r12 = na
var int   side = 0
var float entry = na
var int   n = 0
var int   wins = 0
var float sum = 0.0

if session.isfirstbar_regular
    p10 := na
    p15 := na
    r1 := na
    r12 := na
    side := 0
// the 10:00 am price: the close of the bar that ends at 10:00
if hC == 10 and mC == 0
    p10 := close
    r1 := not na(prevClose) ? (p10 / prevClose - 1) * 100 : na
if hC == 15 and mC == 0
    p15 := close
// the 3:30 pm decision: on the bar that ends at 3:30
sig = hC == 15 and mC == 30 and not na(r1) and r1 != 0
if sig
    r12 := not na(p15) ? (close / p15 - 1) * 100 : na
    agree = not useR12 or (not na(r12) and math.sign(r12) == math.sign(r1))
    side := agree ? (r1 > 0 ? 1 : -1) : 0
    entry := close
    if side != 0
        label.new(bar_index, side == 1 ? low : high, (side == 1 ? "long" : "short") + " 3:30\nr1 " + str.tostring(r1, "+#.00;-#.00") + "%", style = side == 1 ? label.style_label_up : label.style_label_down, color = side == 1 ? color.new(#30d158, 10) : color.new(#ff453a, 10), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
done = session.islastbar_regular and side != 0
if done
    r13 = (close / entry - 1) * 100 * side
    n += 1
    wins += r13 > 0 ? 1 : 0
    sum += r13
    label.new(bar_index, close, str.tostring(r13, "+#.00;-#.00") + "%", style = label.style_label_left, color = r13 > 0 ? color.new(#30d158, 30) : color.new(#ff453a, 30), textcolor = color.white, size = size.tiny)
    side := 0
if session.islastbar_regular
    prevClose := close

bgcolor(hO == 9 ? color.new(#2997ff, 90) : na, title = "First half-hour")
bgcolor(hO == 15 and mO >= 30 ? color.new(#f4b942, 88) : na, title = "Last half-hour")

alertcondition(sig and side == 1, "Intraday momentum: long", "{{ticker}}: first half-hour was up. Long into the close.")
alertcondition(sig and side == -1, "Intraday momentum: short", "{{ticker}}: first half-hour was down. Short into the close.")

var table tb = table.new(position.top_right, 2, 5, bgcolor = color.new(#1d1d1f, 0), border_width = 1, border_color = color.new(#2c2c2e, 0))
if barstate.islast
    ink = color.new(#f5f5f7, 0)
    dim = color.new(#86868b, 0)
    table.cell(tb, 0, 0, "First half-hour (r1)", text_color = dim, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 1, 0, na(r1) ? "after 10:00" : str.tostring(r1, "+#.00;-#.00") + "%", text_color = na(r1) ? ink : r1 > 0 ? color.new(#30d158, 0) : color.new(#ff453a, 0), text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 0, 1, "At 3:30 pm", text_color = dim, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 1, 1, na(r1) ? "–" : r1 > 0 ? "long into the close" : "short into the close", text_color = ink, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 0, 2, "Days on this chart", text_color = dim, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 1, 2, str.tostring(n), text_color = ink, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 0, 3, "Right direction", text_color = dim, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 1, 3, n > 0 ? str.tostring(wins * 100.0 / n, "#.0") + "%" : "–", text_color = ink, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 0, 4, "Average last half-hour", text_color = dim, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tb, 1, 4, n > 0 ? str.tostring(sum / n, "+#.000;-#.000") + "%" : "–", text_color = ink, text_size = size.small)

Try it on paper first.

For a month, write down SPY's change from yesterday's close at 10:00 am, the 3:30 pm price and the 4:00 pm close. Count how often the last half-hour went the first half-hour's way, and what a realistic fill would have cost you.

  • Write the side down at 10:00, before you know the afternoon.
  • Use the actual 3:30 and 4:00 prints.
  • Subtract your commission and half the spread twice.
