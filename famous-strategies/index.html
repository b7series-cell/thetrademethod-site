Stocks · Jegadeesh & Titman, 1993

Cross-Sectional Momentum

Rank every stock on its last 3 to 12 months, buy the top tenth, short the bottom tenth, hold for months and rebalance on a schedule.

Read › Stocks · Bernard & Thomas, 1989

Post-Earnings Drift

After a big earnings surprise, stocks keep drifting the same way for weeks, with jumps at the next reports.

Read › Stocks · Gatev, Goetzmann & Rouwenhorst, 2006

Pairs Trading

Match stocks that moved together for a year; when they split by two standard deviations, short the leader and buy the laggard.

Read › Stocks · Joel Greenblatt

Magic Formula

Rank stocks on earnings yield and return on capital, add the ranks, and hold the best totals for a year.

Read › Stocks · Fama & French, 1992–93

Value and Size

Beta didn't explain returns; size and book-to-market did. Tilt small and cheap, re-sort each June.

Read › Stocks · Ang et al., 2006; Frazzini & Pedersen, 2014

Low-Volatility Anomaly

Calm, low-beta stocks have earned about as much as wild ones, and far more per unit of risk.

Read › Stocks · William O'Neil

CAN SLIM

Seven letters for growth leaders, bought on breakouts from sound bases, every loss cut at 7–8%.

Read › Stocks · Crabel; Zarattini, Barbon, Aziz

Opening Range Breakout

Trade the break of the first 5-minute candle on stocks in play, a 10% ATR stop, out at the close.

Read › ETFs · Mebane Faber

10-Month Moving Average

Once a month: above the 10-month average, hold it; below it, move to T-bills.

Read › ETFs · Gary Antonacci

Dual Momentum

Hold the stronger of US and non-US stocks, but only while US stocks beat T-bills. Otherwise, bonds.

Read › ETFs · Faber; Moskowitz, Grinblatt

Sector Rotation

Rank ten sectors on trailing return each month and hold the top three, with a trend filter for bear markets.

Read › ETFs · Larry Connors

RSI(2)

Above the 200-day, buy when the 2-period RSI drops to 5 or below; sell on the first close above the 5-day average.

Read › ETFs · Gao, Han, Li, Zhou

Intraday Momentum

If SPY is up from yesterday's close at 10:00 am, buy at 3:30 pm; if down, short. Out at the close.

Read › ETFs · Lakonishok, Smidt; McConnell, Xu

Turn of the Month

Own the index only from the close before the month's last trading day to the close of the third day of the next.

Read › ETFs · Cliff, Cooper & Gulen, 2008

Overnight Effect

Own the index only from the close to the next open, and stay flat while the market trades.
