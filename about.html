Who writes this

I'm Bryant. The Trade Method is my trading notebook. When a video, a book, a research paper or another trader's playbook teaches something worth keeping, I write it down here as a plan I could actually follow: the setup, the entry, the stop, the exit and the filters that keep me out of bad trades.

I write every page and I'm the one who answers the contact form. I'm not a registered investment adviser or broker, and nothing here is personal advice. The site exists so I learn the material properly, and so anyone else studying the same ideas can see them laid out step by step.

How a page gets made

  1. Start from a named source. Every setup and fundamentals page names the video, book, paper or trader it comes from and links to it. Where a page adds my own reading of the idea, it says so.
  2. Write the rules as steps. The idea becomes a numbered plan: where the level is, what has to happen before an entry, where the stop goes and how the trade is managed.
  3. Animate it. The charts that walk through each step run on made-up example bars, so you can see the mechanics without a real ticker getting in the way. Worked examples from the source keep their real tickers and numbers.
  4. Build the indicator. Where the rules can be coded, I write a TradingView Pine indicator for them and post it on the page.
  5. Test it and publish the result, good or bad. Pages with a “We tested it” section show what happened when the rules ran on real data, including the ones that lost money or fired too rarely to judge. All of them are collected on Test results.

Updates and corrections

Each page shows the date it last changed. If a number looks wrong, a chart won't load or a step doesn't read right, tell me which page and I'll fix it.
