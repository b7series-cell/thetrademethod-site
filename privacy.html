Privacy

What this site collects.

Visit counts, contact form messages and a few settings in your browser. No ads, no accounts, no selling of data.

The short version

The site counts visits with Google Analytics, keeps the messages you send through the contact form, and saves a few settings in your own browser. There are no ads, no accounts and no selling of data.

Visit counts (Google Analytics)

Pages load Google Analytics 4, which records things like which pages were viewed, how you arrived (a search, a link, an AI answer) and a rough location and device type. Google sets its own cookies to do this. I use the reports to see which pages help people and which need work. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's privacy policy.

To stop your visits being counted in a browser, open any page with ?notrack=1 at the end of the address, for example thetrademethod.com/?notrack=1. That browser stays opted out until you visit with ?notrack=0 or clear the site's data. A browser extension that blocks Google Analytics works too.

The contact form

When you send a message, the form emails it to me and the server keeps a copy with your name, email, topic, the page you sent it from and the message. Your email is only used to reply. To stop abuse the server also keeps a short-lived, one-way scrambled version of your IP address for rate limiting, kept for a day; it can't be turned back into the address.

To have a message you sent deleted, ask through the form using the same email address.

Settings saved in your browser

Some pages remember small choices on your own device, such as the side or time window you picked on a backtest panel, and whether you've opted out of analytics. The site can also be installed as an app, which stores copies of pages on your device so they open offline. None of this is sent anywhere; clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

Other services pages load

  • Google Fonts serves the typeface.
  • YouTube serves the reference videos embedded on some pages, mostly through its privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie.com) player.
  • jsDelivr serves the charting library used on some setup pages.
  • SiteGround hosts the site and keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses.

Each of these sees your IP address when your browser loads from it, under its own privacy policy.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page changes with it. Questions go through the contact form.
