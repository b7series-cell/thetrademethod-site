Fibonacci OTE

What is the Fibonacci OTE?

The Fibonacci Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) is a pullback entry from ICT and the smart money crowd. You draw the Fibonacci retracement on the latest impulse of a clean trend and put a limit order at 0.702, the middle of the 0.618 to 0.786 zone, with the stop past the swing that started the impulse and half off near the old high.

Watch one OTE trade, start to finish

1

Find a clean trend

  • Mark the impulses (big candles of one colour) and the pullbacks (mixed colours, small bodies, wicks).
  • Uptrend: only buys. Downtrend: only sells.
  • Choppy or unclear? Close the chart and look at another one. This works best in clean trends.

Shallow pullbacks mean a strong trend; deeper ones mean it's tiring. Most trends start strong, fade, and turn.

2

Draw the fib on the latest impulse

  • From the start of the move to the end. Bullish: 1 on the swing low, 0 on the swing high. Bearish: the other way round.
  • If price keeps going, move the 0 with it. The tool measures the pullback; it doesn't call the top.
  • A pullback that doesn't reach 0.382 is noise. Treat it as one leg and redraw.

Premium is always the top half of the range, discount the bottom half, whichever way the numbers run.

3

Check the room

  • Switch up a time frame. Is price already inside, or close to, a higher time frame supply zone (or demand, for a short)?
  • That zone can turn price and stop you out. Only take it with enough distance between the entry and that zone.

The second target is that next higher time frame level, so the room is your reward.

4

Set a limit at 0.702

  • Buy limit (sell limit for a short) at 0.702, the middle of the OTE, between 0.618 and 0.786.
  • Let price come to you. If it never reaches the zone, there's no trade, and that's fine.

Deep pullbacks are common in trends. Entering there gets you in earlier and at a better price than waiting for more signals.

5

Stop past the swing

  • Below the swing low that started the impulse for a long; above the swing high for a short.
  • A break of that swing invalidates the trend you were buying.

The deeper the entry, the closer it is to that swing: a smaller stop for the same target.

6

Half off, then let it run

  • First target a little short of the recent high: conservative, with a strong chance of being hit. Close half.
  • Move the stop to break even. The rest is a free trade.
  • Second target: the next important level ahead of price on the higher time frame.

Banking half early pays for the patience the second half needs.

The levels that matter

Written as decimals, the same numbers as the percentages (0.618 is 61.8%).

0.382

The floor for a real pullback. Less than this is noise. 0.382 to 0.5 is where strong trends pull back, but the reward to risk is poor.

0.5

The middle of the range: premium above, discount below. Buy in discount, sell in premium.

0.5–0.618

The Golden Zone. 0.618 is the golden ratio, and price often reacts here.

0.618–0.786

The OTE, with 0.702 in the middle. 0.786 alone gives the best ratio but misses many winners.

When it's off

No trend, no room or no pullback means no trade.

No clean trend

Choppy, overlapping candles, no clear impulse and pullback.

Close the chart. Find another market.

No room

Price is inside or right under a higher time frame supply zone (demand, for a short).

Skip it: that zone can turn it on you.

A shallow pullback

Price turns before 0.618. The order never fills.

No trade. Wait for the next impulse.

Chasing

Buying after the move, or inside 0.382 to 0.5, with the stop still under the swing.

Poor reward to risk. Wait for the pullback.

How deep should you buy?

The stop stays under the swing low and the first target is the old high, so every level deeper you buy shrinks the risk and grows the reward. The catch: the deeper the level, the more often price turns before it gets there. WHY IT WORKS

Everyone's watching

Not because markets obey nature. So many traders watch the same levels that orders pile up there, and price reacts because people expect it to. A self-fulfilling prophecy.

THE RANGE

Fixed after a real pullback

While price keeps pushing, the range keeps moving with it. In the ComLucro framework it's only fixed once price pulls back at least to 0.382. Then the higher low and the next higher high can be marked.

THE DECISION

Break of structure or change of character

Range set, pullback formed, then the market decides. Above the old high is a break of structure: the trend goes on. Lower instead, and a change of character may be forming.

Strategy two: a major level, a double top, then the fib

The same retracement, used to get a better price after a confirmation. Find a major level, drop one time frame to see sellers (or buyers) actually step in, and once the pattern confirms, don't chase it: wait for the pullback to 0.618 of the break.

THE LEVEL

Major market structure

Rejected price more than once, acted as both support and resistance, and was respected recently. Those levels give some of the best entries, but the level alone isn't the trade.

THE CONFIRMATION

Double bottom or top, one time frame down

Two tests that fail to break through: buyers (or sellers) are defending it. It only counts once price breaks the neckline. No break, no trade.

THE ENTRY

Late after the break? Wait

By the time the neckline breaks, the stop past the swing would be huge. Fib the break, set a limit at 0.618 of the OTE, stop past the swing, target the next level.

“More confirmation does not always mean a safer trade.”Com Lucro Trader, on strategy one. Often it just means you got in late.

Missed fills are part of the plan

A limit at 0.702 means some of the best moves leave without you. In the video's second gold setup the pullback never reached 0.618, the order never filled, and the answer was simply to wait for the next one.

How you trade it

Both strategies, in order.

  1. Trend: mark the impulses and pullbacks. Clean uptrend, buys only; clean downtrend, sells only; choppy, next chart.
  2. Fib: on the latest impulse, start to end. Bullish, 1 on the swing low and 0 on the high. Move the 0 while price keeps going; a pullback under 0.382 is noise.
  3. Room: check the higher time frame. Skip it if price is in, or close to, a supply zone (demand, for shorts).
  4. Order: a limit at 0.702, inside the 0.618 to 0.786 OTE, in discount for a buy, premium for a sell.
  5. Stop: past the swing that started the impulse.
  6. Manage: half off a little short of the recent high, stop to break even, the rest at the next higher time frame level.
  7. Strategy two: a major level, a double bottom or top one time frame down, the neckline break, then a limit at 0.618 of the break. Stop past the swing, target the next level.

The indicator

One TradingView script for strategy one (Pine Script v6), for any market and time frame. It finds the break of structure, draws the fib on the latest impulse with the OTE and premium and discount, works the limit at 0.702, and follows the trade to TP1 and TP2. It marks and alerts. It does not place trades. On the video's gold chart it takes the same long (filled at 0.702, about 4,450) and, like the video, skips the next one when the pullback stops short of the order.

  • Fibonacci OTE

    alerts

    Swing highs and lows are pivots (5 bars each side by default). A close through the last swing high sets the trend to buys only; through the last swing low, sells only. The fib goes from the swing the impulse started at (the 1) to its extreme (the 0), and the 0 moves while price keeps going. The order sits at 0.702 (or 0.618 or 0.786, your pick), the stop a few ticks past the 1, TP1 a little short of the old extreme (half, then the stop moves to entry) and TP2 at the nearest higher time frame swing level ahead (4-hour by default), or an extension if there is none. If that level is too close to the 0, the setup is skipped for room. A pullback that reaches 0.382 and then a new extreme without a fill is marked "no fill", and the next fib is drawn from that pullback, as in the video. A break the other way while a trade is open leaves that trade alone; when it closes, the new direction is measured from the swing the break started at. A table shows the trend, the working order and the running count of wins, break evens, losses and no fills.

    "A clean trend" is a judgement call; pivots are a stand-in, so a choppy chart will still draw setups. Close the chart if the waves aren't clear. Strategy two (the double top or bottom at a major level) isn't in the script. The swing strength, the buffer, the TP1 offset, the room and the higher time frame are settings, and the defaults are ours, not the video's.

Questions

What is the OTE zone?

The part of the retracement between 0.618 and 0.786, where price is cheapest for a buyer (discount) or dearest for a seller (premium). This page uses the classic levels; ICT's original is 0.62 to 0.79 with 0.705 in the middle.

Which way do you draw the Fibonacci on the impulse?

For a long, 1 on the swing low and 0 on the swing high; for a short, the other way round. If price keeps going, move the 0 with it. A pullback that doesn't reach 0.382 is noise.

What if price never reaches 0.618?

The limit order never fills and there's no trade. Missed fills are part of the plan: wait for the next impulse.

Does the Fibonacci OTE work?

Not better than chance in our test. Run with the indicator's own rules on the 1-hour chart, the 0.702 limit made +0.02R a trade over 892 trades with a 34% win rate, about the same as random entries on the same impulse with the same stop and targets (−0.01R).
