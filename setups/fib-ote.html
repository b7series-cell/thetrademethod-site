Fibonacci OTE

Tested: No edge vs random

Price moves in waves: an impulse, then a pullback. Don't buy the impulse. Draw the Fibonacci retracement on it and let the pullback come to you, deep in discount, into the Optimal Trade Entry between 0.618 and 0.786. One limit order at 0.702, the stop past the swing, half off at the old high.

Watch one OTE trade, start to finish

1

Find a clean trend

  • Mark the impulses (big candles of one colour) and the pullbacks (mixed colours, small bodies, wicks).
  • Uptrend: only buys. Downtrend: only sells.
  • Choppy or unclear? Close the chart and look at another one. This works best in clean trends.

Shallow pullbacks mean a strong trend; deeper ones mean it's tiring. Most trends start strong, fade, and turn.

2

Draw the fib on the latest impulse

  • From the start of the move to the end. Bullish: 1 on the swing low, 0 on the swing high. Bearish: the other way round.
  • If price keeps going, move the 0 with it. The tool measures the pullback; it doesn't call the top.
  • A pullback that doesn't reach 0.382 is noise. Treat it as one leg and redraw.

Premium is always the top half of the range, discount the bottom half, whichever way the numbers run.

3

Check the room

  • Switch up a time frame. Is price already inside, or close to, a higher time frame supply zone (or demand, for a short)?
  • That zone can turn price and stop you out. Only take it with enough distance between the entry and that zone.

The second target is that next higher time frame level, so the room is your reward.

4

Set a limit at 0.702

  • Buy limit (sell limit for a short) at 0.702, the middle of the OTE, between 0.618 and 0.786.
  • Let price come to you. If it never reaches the zone, there's no trade, and that's fine.

Deep pullbacks are common in trends. Entering there gets you in earlier and at a better price than waiting for more signals.

5

Stop past the swing

  • Below the swing low that started the impulse for a long; above the swing high for a short.
  • A break of that swing invalidates the trend you were buying.

The deeper the entry, the closer it is to that swing: a smaller stop for the same target.

6

Half off, then let it run

  • First target a little short of the recent high: conservative, with a strong chance of being hit. Close half.
  • Move the stop to break even. The rest is a free trade.
  • Second target: the next important level ahead of price on the higher time frame.

Banking half early pays for the patience the second half needs.

The levels that matter

Written as decimals, the same numbers as the percentages (0.618 is 61.8%).

0.382

The floor for a real pullback. Less than this is noise. 0.382 to 0.5 is where strong trends pull back, but the reward to risk is poor.

0.5

The middle of the range: premium above, discount below. Buy in discount, sell in premium.

0.5–0.618

The Golden Zone. 0.618 is the golden ratio, and price often reacts here.

0.618–0.786

The OTE, with 0.702 in the middle. 0.786 alone gives the best ratio but misses many winners.

When it's off

No trend, no room or no pullback means no trade.

No clean trend

Choppy, overlapping candles, no clear impulse and pullback.

Close the chart. Find another market.

No room

Price is inside or right under a higher time frame supply zone (demand, for a short).

Skip it: that zone can turn it on you.

A shallow pullback

Price turns before 0.618. The order never fills.

No trade. Wait for the next impulse.

Chasing

Buying after the move, or inside 0.382 to 0.5, with the stop still under the swing.

Poor reward to risk. Wait for the pullback.

How deep should you buy?

The stop stays under the swing low and the first target is the old high, so every level deeper you buy shrinks the risk and grows the reward. The catch: the deeper the level, the more often price turns before it gets there. Drag the slider, try the presets, or flip it to a bearish fib: the numbers flip, premium and discount never trade places.

0.702
WHY IT WORKS

Everyone's watching

Not because markets obey nature. So many traders watch the same levels that orders pile up there, and price reacts because people expect it to. A self-fulfilling prophecy.

THE RANGE

Fixed after a real pullback

While price keeps pushing, the range keeps moving with it. In the ComLucro framework it's only fixed once price pulls back at least to 0.382. Then the higher low and the next higher high can be marked.

THE DECISION

Break of structure or change of character

Range set, pullback formed, then the market decides. Above the old high is a break of structure: the trend goes on. Lower instead, and a change of character may be forming.

ICT's OTE, and the levels used here

The Optimal Trade Entry comes from ICT and the smart money crowd. It isn't a new tool: it's the same retracement, narrowed to the part of the range where price is cheapest for a buyer (discount) or dearest for a seller (premium).

Bullish: 0 on the swing high, 1 on the swing low. Bearish: 0 on the swing low, 1 on the swing high. The video writes every level as a decimal from the start so the plain retracement and the OTE speak the same language.

Strategy two: a major level, a double top, then the fib

The same retracement, used to get a better price after a confirmation. Find a major level, drop one time frame to see sellers (or buyers) actually step in, and once the pattern confirms, don't chase it: wait for the pullback to 0.618 of the break.

THE LEVEL

Major market structure

Rejected price more than once, acted as both support and resistance, and was respected recently. Those levels give some of the best entries, but the level alone isn't the trade.

THE CONFIRMATION

Double bottom or top, one time frame down

Two tests that fail to break through: buyers (or sellers) are defending it. It only counts once price breaks the neckline. No break, no trade.

THE ENTRY

Late after the break? Wait

By the time the neckline breaks, the stop past the swing would be huge. Fib the break, set a limit at 0.618 of the OTE, stop past the swing, target the next level.

“More confirmation does not always mean a safer trade.”Com Lucro Trader, on strategy one. Often it just means you got in late.

Missed fills are part of the plan

A limit at 0.702 means some of the best moves leave without you. In the video's second gold setup the pullback never reached 0.618, the order never filled, and the answer was simply to wait for the next one.

How you trade it

Both strategies, in order.

  1. Trend: mark the impulses and pullbacks. Clean uptrend, buys only; clean downtrend, sells only; choppy, next chart.
  2. Fib: on the latest impulse, start to end. Bullish, 1 on the swing low and 0 on the high. Move the 0 while price keeps going; a pullback under 0.382 is noise.
  3. Room: check the higher time frame. Skip it if price is in, or close to, a supply zone (demand, for shorts).
  4. Order: a limit at 0.702, inside the 0.618 to 0.786 OTE, in discount for a buy, premium for a sell.
  5. Stop: past the swing that started the impulse.
  6. Manage: half off a little short of the recent high, stop to break even, the rest at the next higher time frame level.
  7. Strategy two: a major level, a double bottom or top one time frame down, the neckline break, then a limit at 0.618 of the break. Stop past the swing, target the next level.

The indicator

One TradingView script for strategy one (Pine Script v6), for any market and time frame. It finds the break of structure, draws the fib on the latest impulse with the OTE and premium and discount, works the limit at 0.702, and follows the trade to TP1 and TP2. It marks and alerts. It does not place trades.

  • Fibonacci OTE

    alerts

    Swing highs and lows are pivots (5 bars each side by default). A close through the last swing high sets the trend to buys only; through the last swing low, sells only. The fib goes from the swing the impulse started at (the 1) to its extreme (the 0), and the 0 moves while price keeps going. The order sits at 0.702 (or 0.618 or 0.786, your pick), the stop a few ticks past the 1, TP1 a little short of the old extreme (half, then the stop moves to entry) and TP2 at the nearest higher time frame swing level ahead (4-hour by default), or an extension if there is none. If that level is too close to the 0, the setup is skipped for room. A pullback that reaches 0.382 and then a new extreme without a fill is marked "no fill", and the next fib is drawn from that pullback, as in the video. A table shows the trend, the working order and the running count of wins, break evens, losses and no fills.

    "A clean trend" is a judgement call; pivots are a stand-in, so a choppy chart will still draw setups. Close the chart if the waves aren't clear. Strategy two (the double top or bottom at a major level) isn't in the script. The swing strength, the buffer, the TP1 offset, the room and the higher time frame are settings, and the defaults are ours, not the video's.

//@version=6
// =====================================================================
// Fibonacci OTE — thetrademethod.com
//
// Strategy one from the Fibonacci OTE page (Com Lucro Trader's
// "Fibonacci Retracement + OTE"), for any market and time frame
// (1-hour in the video):
//
//   1. Trend      Swing highs and lows (pivots). A close above the last
//                 swing high is a break of structure up: buys only. A
//                 close below the last swing low: sells only.
//   2. The fib    On the latest impulse: 1 on the swing it started from
//                 (A), 0 on its end (B). While price keeps making new
//                 extremes, B moves with it.
//   3. Room       Higher time frame swing levels. If the nearest one
//                 ahead of B is too close, the setup is skipped.
//   4. The order  A limit at 0.702, the middle of the OTE (0.618 to
//                 0.786), in discount for a buy, premium for a sell.
//   5. The stop   Past A, by a buffer.
//   6. Targets    TP1 a little short of B (half, then the stop goes to
//                 entry), TP2 the next higher time frame level (or a
//                 fib extension if there is none).
//
//   A pullback that reaches 0.382 fixes the range. If price then makes
//   a new extreme without filling the order, that setup is marked
//   "no fill" and the next impulse is measured from the pullback.
//
//   It marks and alerts. It does not place trades.
// =====================================================================
indicator("Fibonacci OTE [TTM]", shorttitle = "Fib OTE", overlay = true, max_lines_count = 300, max_labels_count = 300, max_boxes_count = 50, max_bars_back = 1000)

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- inputs
grpT = "Trend and swings"
pivLen   = input.int(5, "Swing strength (bars each side)", minval = 1, maxval = 50, group = grpT)
maxLook  = input.int(300, "Look back for the swing (bars)", minval = 20, maxval = 990, group = grpT)

grpF = "Fib and order"
entryLvl = input.float(0.702, "Entry level", options = [0.618, 0.702, 0.786], group = grpF, tooltip = "0.702 is the middle of the OTE, the strategy's limit order. 0.618 fills more often; 0.786 has the best ratio and misses more.")
validLvl = input.float(0.382, "A real pullback reaches", minval = 0.1, maxval = 0.6, step = 0.001, group = grpF, tooltip = "Pullbacks that never reach this are noise: the impulse is still one leg.")
minImp   = input.float(0.0, "Smallest impulse (x ATR 14)", minval = 0, step = 0.5, group = grpF, tooltip = "0 = any size. Skips impulses smaller than this many ATRs.")

grpR = "Risk and targets"
bufTk    = input.int(10, "Stop past the swing by (ticks)", minval = 0, group = grpR)
tp1Pct   = input.float(6.0, "TP1: short of the old extreme by (% of the impulse)", minval = 0, maxval = 50, step = 0.5, group = grpR)
htfIn    = input.timeframe("240", "Higher time frame for room and TP2", group = grpR)
htfLen   = input.int(3, "Higher time frame swing strength", minval = 1, maxval = 20, group = grpR)
roomPct  = input.float(30, "Need room of at least (% of the impulse)", minval = 0, maxval = 300, group = grpR, tooltip = "Distance from B to the nearest higher time frame level ahead. Less than this and the setup is skipped. 0 turns the check off.")
extMult  = input.float(0.618, "TP2 if no level ahead: extension (x impulse)", minval = 0.1, step = 0.1, group = grpR)

grpD = "Display"
showFib  = input.bool(true, "Fib levels", group = grpD)
showPD   = input.bool(true, "Premium and discount shading", group = grpD)
showBos  = input.bool(true, "Break of structure labels", group = grpD)
showTbl  = input.bool(true, "Status table", group = grpD)
extBars  = input.int(15, "Extend lines right (bars)", minval = 0, maxval = 100, group = grpD)
cUp      = input.color(color.new(#30d158, 0), "Long", group = grpD)
cDn      = input.color(color.new(#ff453a, 0), "Short", group = grpD)
cGold    = input.color(color.new(#f4b942, 0), "OTE", group = grpD)
cTgt     = input.color(color.new(#22d3ee, 0), "Targets", group = grpD)

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- helpers
tick = syminfo.mintick
fibAt(float a, float b, float r) => b - r * (b - a)      // a = the 1, b = the 0
fmt(float v) => str.tostring(v, format.mintick)
atr14 = ta.atr(14)

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- swings on the chart
ph = ta.pivothigh(high, pivLen, pivLen)
pl = ta.pivotlow(low, pivLen, pivLen)
var float lastPH = na
var int   lastPHb = na
var float lastPL = na
var int   lastPLb = na
if not na(ph)
    lastPH := ph
    lastPHb := bar_index - pivLen
if not na(pl)
    lastPL := pl
    lastPLb := bar_index - pivLen

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- higher time frame levels
[hPH, hPL] = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, htfIn, [ta.pivothigh(high, htfLen, htfLen), ta.pivotlow(low, htfLen, htfLen)], lookahead = barmerge.lookahead_off)
var array<float> htfHi = array.new<float>()
var array<float> htfLo = array.new<float>()
if not na(hPH) and (htfHi.size() == 0 or htfHi.last() != hPH)
    htfHi.push(hPH)
    if htfHi.size() > 40
        htfHi.shift()
if not na(hPL) and (htfLo.size() == 0 or htfLo.last() != hPL)
    htfLo.push(hPL)
    if htfLo.size() > 40
        htfLo.shift()

// nearest higher time frame level ahead of x (above for d = 1, below for d = -1)
nearestAhead(float x, int d) =>
    float best = na
    if d == 1
        for v in htfHi
            if v > x and (na(best) or v < best)
                best := v
    else
        for v in htfLo
            if v < x and (na(best) or v > best)
                best := v
    best

// extreme over the last n bars (n >= 0), and how many bars back it was
lowestBack(int n) =>
    float m = low
    int k = 0
    for i = 0 to math.min(n, 990)
        if low[i] < m
            m := low[i]
            k := i
    [m, k]
highestBack(int n) =>
    float m = high
    int k = 0
    for i = 0 to math.min(n, 990)
        if high[i] > m
            m := high[i]
            k := i
    [m, k]

// st: 0 = nothing, 1 = order working (the leg), 2 = in the trade
var int   dir   = 0
var int   st    = 0
var float A     = na
var int   aBar  = na
var float B     = na
var int   bBar  = na
var float pbX   = na      // pullback extreme since B
var int   pbBar = na
var bool  valid = false   // pullback reached the 0.382
var float E     = na
var float S     = na
var float T1    = na
var float T2    = na
var bool  room  = true
var bool  hit1  = false
var float stopNow = na

var int nWin = 0
var int nBE  = 0
var int nLoss = 0
var int nMiss = 0

// levels for the current A, B
calcLevels() =>
    e  = fibAt(A, B, entryLvl)
    s  = dir == 1 ? A - bufTk * tick : A + bufTk * tick
    t1 = B - dir * (tp1Pct / 100) * math.abs(B - A)
    lv = nearestAhead(B, dir)
    t2 = na(lv) ? B + dir * extMult * math.abs(B - A) : lv
    rm = roomPct <= 0 or na(lv) or math.abs(lv - B) >= (roomPct / 100) * math.abs(B - A)
    big = minImp <= 0 or math.abs(B - A) >= minImp * atr14
    [e, s, t1, t2, rm and big]

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- break of structure
bosUp = not na(lastPH) and close > lastPH and close[1] <= lastPH
bosDn = not na(lastPL) and close < lastPL and close[1] >= lastPL

evFill  = false
evT1    = false
evT2    = false
evStop  = false
evMiss  = false
evArm   = false
evBos   = 0

if bosUp and dir != 1
    evBos := 1
    if st == 1
        st := 0
    if st != 2
        [lo, k] = lowestBack(math.min(bar_index - lastPHb, maxLook))
        [hi, kh] = highestBack(k)
        dir := 1
        st := 1
        A := lo
        aBar := bar_index - k
        B := hi
        bBar := bar_index - kh
        pbX := na
        pbBar := na
        valid := false
        evArm := true
    else
        dir := 1
    lastPH := na
else if bosDn and dir != -1
    evBos := -1
    if st == 1
        st := 0
    if st != 2
        [hi, k] = highestBack(math.min(bar_index - lastPLb, maxLook))
        [lo, kl] = lowestBack(k)
        dir := -1
        st := 1
        A := hi
        aBar := bar_index - k
        B := lo
        bBar := bar_index - kl
        pbX := na
        pbBar := na
        valid := false
        evArm := true
    else
        dir := -1
    lastPL := na

            aBar := pbBar

        bBar := bar_index
        pbX := na
        pbBar := na

        if na(pbX) or (dir == 1 ? ext < pbX : ext > pbX)
            pbX := ext
            pbBar := bar_index
        if not valid and (dir == 1 ? pbX <= fibAt(A, B, validLvl) : pbX >= fibAt(A, B, validLvl))

Don't chase the impulse. Let the pullback come to you.

The Fibonacci retracement tells you where a pullback may end; the OTE narrows it to the cheapest part of the range for a buyer, or the dearest for a seller. In a clean trend, with room to the next big level, one limit order in that zone and a stop past the swing is the whole trade.

  • A clean trend, or a different chart.
  • Fib the latest impulse; under 0.382 is noise.
  • Buy in discount, sell in premium.
  • Limit at 0.702, stop past the swing.
  • Half at the old high, the rest at the next level.
