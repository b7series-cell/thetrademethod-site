What is ICT trading, made simple?

ICT trading, as Trader Mayne strips it down, is a price action framework in six pieces: the trend, market structure breaks, the range and its 0.5 premium and discount, three points of interest (order blocks, breakers, fair value gaps), and the draw on liquidity. You map it on a high time frame and enter on a low time frame breaker.

Watch one trade, map to trigger

1

Trend: up, down, or sideways

  • Uptrend: higher highs and higher lows. Downtrend: lower highs and lower lows. It should be obvious. If you can't tell, it's sideways.
  • Mark the turning points with three candle swings: a swing high has a lower candle on each side, a swing low a higher candle on each side.
  • The best long in an uptrend is as close to the next higher low as you can get; the best short in a downtrend, the next lower high.

Buy in the middle of the move and you sit through the drawdown before the trend carries on.

2

Structure: a break, or a shift

  • A market structure break (MSB): a candle body closes through the last swing high (low, in a downtrend). A wick doesn't count. The trend continues.
  • A market structure shift (MSS): instead of a new high, price breaks the last higher low. The trend may be turning.
  • After a bullish MSB, the next pullback is expected to make a higher low. Don't turn bearish on it.

Structure just describes the trend, one confirmed swing at a time.

3

Range: the current leg, low to high

  • From the significant low before the break to the significant high after it: the obvious ones, with a clear reaction off each.
  • A Fibonacci tool set to 0, 0.5 and 1. Above the 0.5 is premium (expensive: sell). Below is discount (cheap: buy).
  • Optional: 0.618 to 0.786 is ICT's optimal trade entry, with the 0.705 sweet spot in the middle. A new MSB starts a new range.

The deeper the discount, the closer the invalidation (the range low), and the better the reward to risk.

4

Points of interest: only three

  • Order block: the down move before the move up that broke structure (the up move before a break down). Often the deep pullback that continues the trend.
  • Breaker: an order block that failed and flipped, support to resistance or back. Often the reversal.
  • Fair value gap: three candles, top of candle one to bottom of candle three, where price only went one way. Often the shallow pullback in a strong trend.

Several at one spot? A good sign. Can't pick? Take the one in the discount for a long, the premium for a short.

5

Liquidity: external and internal

  • External range liquidity (ERL): outside the range, at the range high, the range low and older swings beyond them.
  • Internal range liquidity (IRL): inside the range, mostly the order blocks, breakers and fair value gaps.
  • The draw on liquidity: ask what just got taken. External? The next draw is internal. Internal? The next draw is external.

Liquidity is just orders. Buyers show up at demand inside the range; they sell to the stops and breakout shorts above the old high.

6

Zoom in: the breaker entry

  • Map on the high time frame. Only when price reaches the POI, drop to the low time frame. It'll be trending against you: that's the pullback.
  • Wait for a low to form, a high, then that low taken out. A close through the high that took it is the MSS; the up candles before the drop are the bullish breaker.
  • Enter at the breaker, stop under the low before the turn, target external range liquidity. Take a chunk off before it if you like.

Same idea, much tighter stop. Stopped out but the range low holds? You can try again deeper in the discount.

Two ways to take it

Both start from the same high time frame map.

High time frame only

Limit at the POI, stop past the range low (high), target the range high (low). Simple, but the stop is wide and the R can be thin.

Zoomed in

At the POI, wait for the low time frame breaker. A tighter stop, a bigger R, and room to try more than once.

OB, breaker or FVG?

Deep pullback: order block. Shallow, strong trend: fair value gap. Trend turning: breaker.

Draw on liquidity

Your daily bias. Just took external? Internal is next. Just took internal? External is next.

When it's off

One missing piece means no trade.

No obvious trend

You can't call it higher highs or lower lows at a glance. It's sideways, and the framework needs a trend.

Wait for one.

No POI

The pullback stops in the middle of nowhere, with no order block, breaker or gap. Or it never pulls back at all.

No trade for you.

No low time frame flip

Price is at the POI but the low time frame keeps trending against you, or nukes straight through.

Keep waiting.

Range broken

Price goes through the range low (high, for a short). No more higher lows: the trend has shifted.

Idea's wrong. Redraw.

The three points of interest

ICT and the rest of the internet teach dozens of these. They're all supply and demand, renamed. Three are enough. Each has a bullish and a bearish version.

POIBullishBearishUsually
Order block (OB)The down candle or down move before a move up that breaks structure. Combine consecutive down candles if you like: three red weeklies are one red three-week candle.The up candle or up move before a move down that breaks structure.Continuation, on a deep pullback.
Breaker block (BB)A bearish order block that fails: price blasts through it instead of rejecting, and it becomes support.A bullish order block that fails and becomes resistance.Reversal, at tops and bottoms, with a market structure shift. Not always.
Fair value gap (FVG)Three candles with a big middle one: from the top of candle one to the bottom of candle three, price only traded up.From the bottom of candle one to the top of candle three, price only traded down.Shallow pullbacks in a strong trend.
TIEBREAKER

Discount for longs, premium for shorts

An FVG above the 0.5 and an order block below it? In an uptrend, the one in the discount is the preferred one. Price won't always go that deep for you.

STACKED

An OB, a breaker and an FVG together

When they pile up at one price, it's a strong sign you've found a high probability area for the turn.

NOT A SIGNAL

An area, not an entry

A POI is where the higher low (or lower high) is most likely to form. Price may never reach it, or may nuke right through.

Reps

Easy in hindsight. Pattern recognition takes screen time.

  • Levels and ranges get invalidated. Sometimes the pullback never comes.
  • Start with trades in line with the high time frame trend; counter-trend moves to internal liquidity are valid, but later.

How you trade it

Start to finish, on whatever pair of time frames you trade.

  1. Trend: on the high time frame, is it obviously making higher highs and higher lows, or lower highs and lower lows? If not, wait.
  2. Structure: wait for a body to close through the last swing (an MSB). Now the pullback is a pullback.
  3. Range: once the new high forms, pull the fib from the significant low to it. Note the 0.5 and, if you like, 0.618 to 0.786.
  4. POI: find the order block, breaker or fair value gap in the leg that broke structure. Prefer the one in the discount (premium for shorts).
  5. Liquidity: external was just taken, so the draw is the POI (internal). From there, it's external again: the range high.
  6. Zoom in: when price reaches the POI, drop to the low time frame. Wait for a low, a high, that low taken, then a close through the high: enter at the breaker.
  7. Stop and target: stop under the low before the turn, target external range liquidity, take a chunk off before it if you like. Stopped but the range holds? Look for the next one deeper.

//                 side, a low with a higher one). Up, down, or sideways

//                 break up (MSB, or MSS if the trend was down). A close

//                 price reaches); several on top of each other = stacked.
//   5. Liquidity  External was just taken by the break, so the draw is

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- the map (runs on the high time frame)
// true if no close went through the zone from offset upTo down to 0
heldSince(int upTo, float t, float b, int dir) =>
    bool ok = true
    if upTo >= 0
        for k = 0 to upTo
            if dir == 1 ? close[k] < b : close[k] > t
                ok := false
                break
    ok

htfMap(int n) =>
    var int   tr     = 0          // 1 up, -1 down, 0 sideways (no break yet)
    var float sh     = na
    var int   shB    = na
    var bool  shUsed = true
    var float sl     = na
    var int   slB    = na
    var bool  slUsed = true
    var float rLo    = na
    var float rHi    = na
    var int   rLoB   = na
    var int   rHiB   = na
    var int   brkSw  = na         // bar of the swing that was broken
    var int   brkBar = na         // bar that broke it
    var bool  legMss = false
    var int   ev     = 0          // 1 MSB up, 2 MSS up, -1 MSB down, -2 MSS down
    var int   legT   = na
    float ph = ta.pivothigh(high, n, n)
    float pl = ta.pivotlow(low, n, n)
    if not na(ph)
        sh := ph
        shB := bar_index - n
        shUsed := false
    if not na(pl)
        sl := pl
        slB := bar_index - n
        slUsed := false

    if not shUsed and close > sh
        // a body closed through the last swing high: the range runs from the low before it
        shUsed := true
        int len = math.min(bar_index - shB, 400)
        float mn = low
        int mo = 0
        for i = 0 to len
            if low[i] < mn
                mn := low[i]
                mo := i
        legMss := tr == -1
        ev := tr == -1 ? 2 : 1
        tr := 1
        rLo := mn
        rLoB := bar_index - mo
        rHi := high
        rHiB := bar_index
        brkSw := shB
        brkBar := bar_index
        legT := time[mo]
    else if not slUsed and close < sl
        slUsed := true
        int len = math.min(bar_index - slB, 400)
        float mx = high
        int mo = 0
        for i = 0 to len
            if high[i] > mx
                mx := high[i]
                mo := i
        legMss := tr == 1
        ev := tr == 1 ? -2 : -1
        tr := -1
        rHi := mx
        rHiB := bar_index - mo
        rLo := low
        rLoB := bar_index
        brkSw := slB
        brkBar := bar_index
        legT := time[mo]
    else
        if tr == 1 and high > rHi
            rHi := high
            rHiB := bar_index
        if tr == -1 and low < rLo
            rLo := low
            rLoB := bar_index

    // the points of interest in the current leg
    float eq = (rHi + rLo) / 2
    array<float> zt = array.new<float>()
    array<float> zb = array.new<float>()
    array<int>   zk = array.new<int>()      // 1 FVG, 2 order block, 3 breaker
    if tr != 0 and not na(rLoB) and not na(rHiB)
        int legStart = tr == 1 ? rLoB : rHiB
        int len = math.min(bar_index - legStart, 300)
        float mnC = 1e100
        float mxC = -1e100
        for i = 0 to len
            if i + 2 <= len
                if tr == 1 and low[i] > high[i + 2] and mnC > high[i + 2]
                    zt.push(low[i])
                    zb.push(high[i + 2])
                    zk.push(1)
                if tr == -1 and high[i] < low[i + 2] and mxC < low[i + 2]
                    zt.push(low[i + 2])
                    zb.push(high[i])
                    zk.push(1)
            mnC := math.min(mnC, close[i])
            mxC := math.max(mxC, close[i])
        // order block: the last opposite candle at the leg's low (high)
        int o = bar_index - legStart
        if o <= 400
            for j = o to o + 5
                if tr == 1 ? close[j] < open[j] : close[j] > open[j]
                    if heldSince(j - 1, high[j], low[j], tr)
                        zt.push(high[j])
                        zb.push(low[j])
                        zk.push(2)
                    break
        // breaker: after a shift, the candle before the move that took the low (high)
        if legMss and not na(brkSw)
            int o2 = bar_index - brkSw
            if o2 <= 400
                for j = o2 to o2 + 5
                    if tr == 1 ? close[j] > open[j] : close[j] < open[j]
                        if heldSince(math.min(bar_index - brkBar, 400), high[j], low[j], tr)
                            zt.push(high[j])
                            zb.push(low[j])
                            zk.push(3)
                        break

    float pT = na
    float pB = na
    int   pK = 0
    int   stk = 0
    int   prem = 0
    int nZ = zt.size()
    if nZ > 0
        int best = -1
        for q = 0 to nZ - 1
            bool good = tr == 1 ? zt.get(q) <= eq : zb.get(q) >= eq
            if good and (best < 0 or (tr == 1 ? zt.get(q) > zt.get(best) : zb.get(q) < zb.get(best)))
                best := q
        if best < 0
            prem := 1
            for q = 0 to nZ - 1
                if best < 0 or (tr == 1 ? zt.get(q) < zt.get(best) : zb.get(q) > zb.get(best))
                    best := q
        pT := zt.get(best)
        pB := zb.get(best)
        pK := zk.get(best)
        for q = 0 to nZ - 1
            if q != best and zk.get(q) != pK and zt.get(q) >= pB and zb.get(q) <= pT
                stk += 1

Questions

What is a market structure break?

A candle body closing through the last swing high in an uptrend (or swing low in a downtrend). A wick doesn't count. A market structure shift is price breaking the last higher low instead, a sign the trend may be turning.

What are premium and discount?

Draw a Fibonacci from the significant low to the significant high of the current leg. Above the 0.5 is premium, where you sell; below it is discount, where you buy. 0.618 to 0.786 is the optimal trade entry.

What are the three points of interest?

An order block (the move before the move that broke structure), a breaker (an order block that failed and flipped), and a fair value gap (three candles where price only went one way).

How do you enter with two time frames?

When price reaches the high time frame point of interest, zoom in and wait for the low time frame to flip back in line. Enter on its breaker, stop under the turn, and target external range liquidity.

Map it high. Time it low.

The high time frame tells you where the higher low should form and where price is drawn next. The low time frame tells you when it actually turned. Wait for both, enter on the breaker, and let it run to the liquidity outside the range.

  • Trend: obvious, or sideways.
  • Structure: a close through the swing.
  • Range: low to high, the 0.5, discount.
  • POI: order block, breaker, FVG.
  • Draw: internal, then external.
  • Zoom in: low taken, high broken, breaker.
