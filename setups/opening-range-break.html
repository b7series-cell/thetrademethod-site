What is an opening range breakout?

An opening range breakout (ORB) uses the high and low of the first 15 or 30 minutes of trading. Once that range has fully formed, you enter when price breaks it on real volume with the higher time frame agreeing, put the stop a few cents past the breakout bar, and take profits in layers, often at 1 and 2 ATR.

Watch one opening range break

1

Let the opening range fully form

  • The opening range is simply the high and low of the first 15 or 30 minutes of trading — pick one window and stay consistent with it.
  • Don't take the break before that window has actually finished. Jumping the gun early is a low-win-rate habit.

Big players build positions inside that window, creating real balance. Break it early and you're trading noise before the level even exists.

2

Check the higher time frame first

  • The best orb trades agree with something bigger — a higher-time-frame breakdown, clear relative strength, a fresh catalyst, or a stock printing its first red day after a big run.
  • Look for a real reason: a key level lost or reclaimed, news, elevated short interest, or the exact same pattern repeating from a prior session.

The same opening-range break means very different things on a stock with no story behind it versus one that just lost a multi-month support level or gapped on real news.

3

Confirm with volume

  • You want real volume on the break, not price just ticking through the level. The bigger the outlier, the more confidence you can have.
  • When relative volume runs extreme, give the trade room in your head, not just on your chart — the move can run two, three, even five ATRs.

Volume is what separates a real breakout from price drifting through a line. Confluence between price and volume is the whole signal.

4

Enter on the break, stop off the breakout bar

  • Enter as the range high or low actually breaks, ideally with confluence — a round number, a recent high, a higher-time-frame level, an after-hours high all stacking on one price.
  • Stop goes a few cents beyond the high or low of the actual breakout bar — not an arbitrary distance from the range.

The breakout bar is what proved the trade. If price comes back through it, the thing that triggered you in the first place already failed.

5

Take profits in layers, not all at once

  • Common targets: 1 ATR from the high or low of day for the first scale, 2 ATR when relative volume is running extreme, or scaling into momentum as it visibly slows.
  • A break of the 9 or 21 EMA, a 2-minute trailing stop, or a capitulation volume bar plus a trend break are all valid exits — most experienced traders combine two or three of these rather than leaning on just one.

Different days call for different exits. A single fixed target leaves easy money on strong days and gives back too much on quiet ones.

6

Give the idea more than one attempt

  • A first failed break doesn't kill the idea. Price often fails once or twice at a level before the real move comes.
  • Set an alert at the level instead of staring at the screen, and treat a repeated failure at the same spot as information, not a reason to walk away.

An opening range break that already worked the day before, or has already failed and rebid a couple of times, is showing you the same players are still there — that's higher confidence, not lower.

The same setup, three different flavors

These read as different trades. They're the same trade, with different amounts of confirmation stacked on top.

Tight range, big confluence

Recent highs, whole numbers, and a higher-time-frame level all stacking on one price. The highest-confidence version.

Failed once, then worked

A level can fail on the first or second touch and still resolve into a clean break on the next attempt.

Repeats the prior day

The exact same pattern playing out again the next session, almost tick for tick, is a confidence signal, not a coincidence.

The four filters

These don't find the trade — the range and the break do that. They keep you off the versions that only look like it.

1  Range not fully formed

The 15 or 30-minute window has to actually close before a break of it means anything.

Kills: entering before the range even exists.

2  No higher-time-frame story

The identical price pattern with no catalyst, no relative strength, and no context behind it is a much weaker trade.

Kills: taking the setup in a vacuum.

3  Weak or average volume

A break on volume that looks like every other bar that day isn't telling you anything new.

Kills: mistaking drift for a breakout.

4  Stop measured from the wrong point

The stop belongs a few cents beyond the breakout bar's own high or low — not a round distance from the range.

Kills: guessing at a stop instead of reading it off the bar that triggered you.

Size off the breakout bar, not the range

The range tells you where to watch. The breakout bar tells you where you're wrong. Numbers below are illustrative, built around the OKLO example.

Risk budget for the trade$500.00
Entry — break of the opening range high48.65
Stop — a few cents below the breakout bar47.90
Risk per share$0.75
Position size666 shares

The mistake this prevents

Use the opening range low as your stop instead — say, 46.00 — and you measure risk as 48.65 − 46.00 = $2.65, sizing for 188 shares.

Fine on paper, but you're now holding through a 2.65-point drawdown on a trade the breakout bar itself already invalidated at 47.90.

The range tells you where to watch. It was never meant to be the stop.

When the exact same pattern repeats, take it more seriously

A fifth example from the reference video, on SMCI, worth calling out on its own.

SMCI blew off and closed near its lows on heavy volume one session, failed at two-day VWAP (~61) in the pre-market, then opened below the prior day's close for the first time since its uptrend began — a classic first red day after an overextension. The next morning, the identical opening-range-break-to-the-downside setup that had worked the previous session played out again, almost tick for tick: a push into pre-market resistance near 59.50, a failure, and a breakdown through the range low on the second-highest volume bar of the day. The exit even matched the day before — a two-minute bar break to the upside. A setup that already worked yesterday, on the same stock, in the same spot, is telling you the same participants are still active. That's a reason for more confidence, not less.

Example: an opening range break, start to finish

An example session run through the indicator's own rules. The first 15 minutes are boxed, then the 10:00 bar closes above the range high on more than 2.5× its 20-bar average volume: that close is the buy, with the stop 5 cents under the breakout bar. Half comes off at 1 daily ATR from the low of day, and the rest rides the 9 EMA until a close below it in the afternoon. The bars are made up to show the setup clearly. They aren't a real ticker.

The indicator

One TradingView script for this setup (Pine Script v6), for US stocks and ETFs on a 1- to 15-minute chart. It boxes the opening range, numbers every break attempt, checks the volume, marks the entry and the stop off the breakout bar, and follows the trade to 1 ATR and the 9 EMA. It marks and alerts. It does not place trades.

  • Opening Range Break

    alerts

    The first 15 minutes (or 30) of the regular session are boxed, and nothing trades until that window has closed. A bar that trades through the range high or low is a break attempt: if it closes back inside it's marked "no trade", and if its volume is under 1.5 times the average of the 20 bars before it, "low vol". A close outside on real volume is the entry, with the stop 5 cents past the breakout bar. Half comes off at 1 daily ATR from the low of day (high of day for a short); the rest exits on the first close through the 9 EMA, or at the 4:00 close. A target the day has already used is "no room". One long and one short a day at most. A table shows the range, the trade and the running win rate and average R.

    These are the same rules as the example chart above and our test below. The higher time frame story (a catalyst, relative strength, a lost level) can't be coded, so it isn't filtered: check it yourself before you take a break. The window, the volume multiple, the stop buffer, the ATR target and the EMA are settings. Use regular trading hours on the chart.

//@version=6
// =====================================================================
// Opening Range Break — thetrademethod.com
// https://thetrademethod.com/setups/opening-range-break.html
//
// The rules from the Opening Range Break setup page, the same ones the
// page's example chart and our test use:
//
//   1. The range    High and low of the first 15 minutes of the regular
//                   session (9:30 to 9:45 ET), or 30 minutes. Nothing is
//                   traded until the window has fully closed.
//   2. The break    A bar trades through the range high (long) or low
//                   (short). If it closes back inside, it's a range test:
//                   marked "no trade", and the idea stays alive.
//   3. Volume       The break bar's volume must be at least 1.5 x the
//                   average of the 20 bars before it, or it's "low vol".
//   4. Entry/stop   Entry at the close of the confirmed break bar. Stop
//                   5 cents past the breakout bar's own low (long) or high
//                   (short), not the other side of the range.
//   5. Targets      Half off at 1 daily ATR(14) from the low of day (long)
//                   or high of day (short). If that is already behind the
//                   entry, the day has used its ATR: "no room". The other
//                   half trails the 9 EMA and exits on the first close
//                   through it. Anything open is flat at the 4:00 close.
//   6. Attempts     Every break try is numbered. A failed first try
//                   doesn't kill the idea: the second one can be the
//                   trade. One long and one short a day at most, one
//                   trade at a time.
//
//   The higher time frame story (a catalyst, relative strength, a key
//   level lost) is a judgement call and is NOT filtered. Check it
//   yourself before you take a break.
//
//   Use a 1- to 15-minute chart of a US stock or ETF. Signals are taken
//   on closed bars only. It marks and alerts. It does not place trades.
// =====================================================================
indicator("Opening Range Break [TTM]", shorttitle = "ORB [TTM]", overlay = true, max_lines_count = 500, max_labels_count = 500, max_boxes_count = 500)

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- inputs
grpR = "Range and break"
orMin  = input.int(15, "Opening range (minutes)", options = [15, 30], group = grpR, tooltip = "Pick one window and stay with it. The page uses 15 or 30.")
volX   = input.float(1.5, "Break volume at least (x the average)", minval = 1.0, step = 0.1, group = grpR, tooltip = "The page asks for real, outlier volume. 1.5 is our objective stand-in; raise it to 2 or more for only the biggest outliers.")
volN   = input.int(20, "Volume average: bars before the break", minval = 5, maxval = 200, group = grpR)

grpT = "Stop and targets"
pad    = input.float(0.05, "Stop: past the breakout bar by (price)", minval = 0, step = 0.01, group = grpT, tooltip = "A few cents beyond the breakout bar's own low or high.")
atrLen = input.int(14, "Daily ATR length", minval = 2, group = grpT)
atrX   = input.float(1.0, "First target (x daily ATR from the low / high of day)", minval = 0.25, step = 0.25, group = grpT, tooltip = "1 ATR is the page's first scale. Use 2 when relative volume is extreme.")
emaLen = input.int(9, "Trail the rest on this EMA", minval = 2, group = grpT)

grpS = "Show"
showFail  = input.bool(true, "Mark skipped breaks (no trade, low vol, no room)", group = grpS)
showTable = input.bool(true, "Status table", group = grpS)
cUp   = input.color(#30d158, "Long", group = grpS)
cDn   = input.color(#ff453a, "Short", group = grpS)
cBox  = input.color(#2997ff, "Opening range", group = grpS)
cEma  = input.color(#f4b942, "9 EMA", group = grpS)

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- time
TZ        = "America/New_York"
RTH_OPEN  = 570      // 9:30
RTH_CLOSE = 960      // 16:00
okTf   = timeframe.isintraday and timeframe.in_seconds() <= 900
barMin = int(timeframe.in_seconds() / 60)
mod    = hour(time, TZ) * 60 + minute(time, TZ)
inRth  = mod >= RTH_OPEN and mod < RTH_CLOSE
orEnd  = RTH_OPEN + orMin

// daily ATR through the last completed day (no look-ahead: [1] with lookahead on)
dAtr = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "D", ta.atr(atrLen)[1], lookahead = barmerge.lookahead_on)

var int   dayKey  = na
var bool  dayOk   = false
var float orh     = na
var float orl     = na
var float lod     = na
var float hod     = na
var float dayAtr  = na
var int   triesL  = 0
var int   triesS  = 0
var bool  tookL   = false
var bool  tookS   = false
var box   orBox   = na
var line  hiLn    = na
var line  loLn    = na
var float prevC   = na
var float e9      = na
var array<float> vq = array.new<float>()

var int   side  = 0          // 1 long, -1 short, 0 flat
var int   eBar  = na
var float eP    = na
var float sl    = na
var float tp    = na
var float risk  = na
var bool  half  = false
var float halfX = na
var line  eLn   = na
var line  sLn   = na
var line  tLn   = na

var int   nTr  = 0
var int   nWin = 0
var float sumR = 0.0
var string status = "Waiting for 9:30"
var string lastTrade = "none yet"

bool evRange = false
bool evTry   = false
bool evBuy   = false
bool evSell  = false
bool evHalf  = false
bool evExit  = false

fmt(float p) => str.tostring(p, format.mintick)

// ---------------------------------------------------------------- the rules (closed RTH bars only)
if okTf and inRth and barstate.isconfirmed
    key = year(time, TZ) * 10000 + month(time, TZ) * 100 + dayofmonth(time, TZ)
    lastBar = mod + barMin >= RTH_CLOSE

    // 20-bar volume average BEFORE this bar, and the EMA of regular-session closes
    float vavg = array.size(vq) >= volN ? array.avg(vq) : na
    array.push(vq, volume)
    if array.size(vq) > volN
        array.shift(vq)
    e9 := na(e9) ? close : e9 + (close - e9) * 2.0 / (emaLen + 1)

    // a new session
    if na(dayKey) or key != dayKey
        if side != 0                       // data gap: flatten at this open
            float s0 = side
            float r0 = half ? 0.5 * s0 * (halfX - eP) / risk + 0.5 * s0 * (open - eP) / risk : s0 * (open - eP) / risk
            nTr += 1
            nWin += r0 > 0 ? 1 : 0
            sumR += r0
            side := 0
        dayKey := key
        dayOk  := mod == RTH_OPEN
        orh := na
        orl := na
        lod := na
        hod := na
        dayAtr := dAtr
        triesL := 0
        triesS := 0
        tookL := false
        tookS := false
        orBox := na
        hiLn := na
        loLn := na
        status := dayOk ? "Opening range forming" : "No 9:30 bar: no range today"

    // exits, from the bar after entry: stop first, then target, then the EMA, then the close
    float xPx = na
    string xWhy = ""
    if side != 0 and bar_index > eBar
        bool L = side == 1
        float stopX = L ? (open <= sl ? open : low <= sl ? sl : na) : (open >= sl ? open : high >= sl ? sl : na)
        if not na(stopX)
            xPx := stopX
            xWhy := "Stop"
        else
            if not half
                float tX = L ? (open >= tp ? open : high >= tp ? tp : na) : (open <= tp ? open : low <= tp ? tp : na)
                if not na(tX)
                    half := true
                    halfX := tX
                    evHalf := true
                    label.new(bar_index, L ? high : low, "½ at " + str.tostring(atrX) + " ATR", style = L ? label.style_label_down : label.style_label_up, color = color.new(cUp, 15), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
            if half and (L ? close < e9 : close > e9)
                xPx := close
                xWhy := str.tostring(emaLen) + " EMA exit"
            else if lastBar
                xPx := close
                xWhy := "Close"
        if not na(xPx)
            float s1 = side
            float full = s1 * (xPx - eP) / risk
            float r = half ? 0.5 * s1 * (halfX - eP) / risk + 0.5 * full : full
            nTr += 1
            nWin += r > 0 ? 1 : 0
            sumR += r
            evExit := true
            lastTrade := (side == 1 ? "Long " : "Short ") + (r >= 0 ? "+" : "") + str.tostring(r, "#.##") + "R (" + xWhy + ")"
            label.new(bar_index, L ? high : low, xWhy + "\n" + (r >= 0 ? "+" : "") + str.tostring(r, "#.##") + "R", style = L ? label.style_label_down : label.style_label_up, color = color.new(xWhy == "Stop" ? cDn : r > 0 ? cUp : color.gray, 15), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
            line.set_x2(eLn, bar_index)
            line.set_x2(sLn, bar_index)
            line.set_x2(tLn, bar_index)
            side := 0
            status := "Flat. " + lastTrade
        else if side != 0
            line.set_x2(eLn, bar_index)
            line.set_x2(sLn, bar_index)
            line.set_x2(tLn, bar_index)

    lod := na(lod) ? low : math.min(lod, low)
    hod := na(hod) ? high : math.max(hod, high)

    if dayOk
        if mod < orEnd
            // the range is still forming
            orh := na(orh) ? high : math.max(orh, high)
            orl := na(orl) ? low : math.min(orl, low)
            if na(orBox)
                orBox := box.new(bar_index, orh, bar_index, orl, border_color = color.new(cBox, 40), bgcolor = color.new(cBox, 88))
            else
                box.set_top(orBox, orh)
                box.set_bottom(orBox, orl)
                box.set_right(orBox, bar_index)
            if mod + barMin >= orEnd
                evRange := true
                hiLn := line.new(bar_index, orh, bar_index, orh, color = cBox, width = 2)
                loLn := line.new(bar_index, orl, bar_index, orl, color = color.new(#8e8e93, 0), width = 2)
                status := "Range set " + fmt(orh) + " – " + fmt(orl) + ". Waiting for a break"
        else if na(orh)
            dayOk := false
        else
            line.set_x2(hiLn, bar_index)
            line.set_x2(loLn, bar_index)
            float vr = na(vavg) or vavg == 0 ? na : volume / vavg
            bool volOk = not na(vr) and vr >= volX
            for k = 0 to 1
                bool Lg = k == 0
                float lvl = Lg ? orh : orl
                bool tried = Lg ? high > orh and prevC <= orh : low < orl and prevC >= orl
                if tried
                    int tries = 0
                    if Lg
                        triesL += 1
                        tries := triesL
                    else
                        triesS += 1
                        tries := triesS
                    evTry := true
                    bool outside = Lg ? close > lvl : close < lvl
                    float tgt = Lg ? lod + atrX * dayAtr : hod - atrX * dayAtr
                    string kind = not outside ? "closed back inside · no trade" : not volOk ? "low vol " + (na(vr) ? "" : str.tostring(vr, "#.#") + "x") + " · no trade" : (Lg ? tookL : tookS) ? "side already traded" : side != 0 ? "already in a trade" : lastBar ? "last bar · no trade" : na(dayAtr) ? "no daily ATR" : (Lg ? tgt <= close : tgt >= close) ? "no room to " + str.tostring(atrX) + " ATR" : "go"
                    string tryTxt = tries > 1 ? " · try " + str.tostring(tries) : ""
                    if kind == "go"
                        side := Lg ? 1 : -1
                        eBar := bar_index
                        eP := close
                        sl := Lg ? low - pad : high + pad
                        tp := tgt
                        risk := math.abs(close - sl)
                        half := false
                        halfX := na
                        if Lg
                            tookL := true
                            evBuy := true
                        else
                            tookS := true
                            evSell := true
                        color c = Lg ? cUp : cDn
                        label.new(bar_index, Lg ? low : high, (Lg ? "BUY " : "SELL ") + fmt(close) + "\n" + str.tostring(vr, "#.#") + "x vol" + tryTxt, style = Lg ? label.style_label_up : label.style_label_down, color = c, textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
                        eLn := line.new(bar_index, eP, bar_index, eP, color = color.new(cBox, 0), style = line.style_dashed)
                        sLn := line.new(bar_index, sl, bar_index, sl, color = color.new(cDn, 0), style = line.style_dashed)
                        tLn := line.new(bar_index, tp, bar_index, tp, color = color.new(cUp, 0), style = line.style_dashed)
                        status := (Lg ? "Long " : "Short ") + fmt(eP) + ", stop " + fmt(sl) + ", half at " + fmt(tp)
                    else if showFail

Questions

15 or 30-minute opening range?

Either works. Pick one window, stay consistent with it, and don't take the break before that window has finished.

Where does the stop go on an opening range breakout?

A few cents beyond the high or low of the actual breakout bar, not an arbitrary distance from the range. If price comes back through that bar, the thing that triggered the trade has failed.

What does the higher time frame add?

The best breaks agree with something bigger: a key level lost or reclaimed, a fresh catalyst, clear relative strength, or a first red day after a big run.

Does the opening range breakout work?
