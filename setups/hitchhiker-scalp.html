The Hitchhiker Scalp

You can be right about the direction and still lose money, because you chased. Let the stock show you the momentum first, then wait for the first pullback to the 9 EMA on the 2-minute chart, on lighter volume. Get in as it slows, out on a 2-minute close past the 21 EMA, pay yourself 70% into momentum and trail the rest.

1

Start with a stock in play

  • Abnormal volume, a large trading range, and a reason traders are paying attention.
  • The reason can be fresh news, earnings, a strong sector theme, or an important technical breakout or breakdown level.
  • Not a stock moving around randomly in the middle of a trading range. You want real momentum.

If there's no momentum, there's nothing to hitchhike.

2

Let it make the outsized move, and let it go

  • Several large candles in one direction, with volume expanding. One side is clearly in control.
  • Most newer traders want in right now. Don't, at least not at those prices.
  • After an outsized move the stock is extended from its short-term moving averages. If you got in at the extreme, where are you wrong?

A stop far enough away to survive the pullback makes the trade no longer make sense, especially for a scalp.

3

Wait for the first pullback toward the 9 EMA

  • On the 2-minute chart. For a short it's the first bounce up toward the 9 EMA; for a long, the first dip down to it.
  • It should be controlled: small bodies, momentum slowing, and volume lighter than the move. Not a bounce reclaiming level after level.
  • Better if the 9 EMA lines up with something else: old support now resistance, or a higher time frame level.

The first pullback is a test. Can the other side actually reverse the move, or is this just a pause before the trend takes over again?

4

Enter as the pullback slows

  • Not just because price touched the 9 EMA. You want the counter move having trouble going any further.
  • On a short: the bounce stalls, price rolls over and starts acting weak again. On a long: the selling dries up and buyers step back in.
  • Ideally volume comes back as the original direction resumes. That's the confirmation.

You're not trying to catch the very beginning of the move. You want the next leg, from a better entry with a clearer place to be wrong.

5

Stop on a 2-minute close past the 21 EMA

  • Short: a 2-minute candle closing above the 21 EMA is the exit. Long: a 2-minute close below it.
  • You know what invalidates the setup before you enter, so you can calculate the position size.
  • Once it works, with a significant move or a new low (or high), tighten the stop to the most recent high on a short, the most recent low on a long.

A defined rule and stop. You're not sitting there deciding how much pain you're willing to take.

6

Pay yourself 70%, trail the last 30%

  • Take around 70% of the position off into momentum, in small pieces, sometimes about 5% at a time.
  • Trail the remaining 30% on the prior 2-minute bar: on a short, a break of the prior bar's high is the exit; on a long, a break of its low.
  • You don't need it to trend for the rest of the day. You need a clean entry, defined risk, and the next leg.

This is a scalp. You're not trying to predict the exact low. You're trying to capture the move.

What "in play" means

Three things, all at once. Without them there's no momentum to ride.

Abnormal volume

Trading a lot more than it normally does. Traders are paying attention to it today.

A large range

It's actually moving, with room for a next leg after the first pullback.

A reason

Fresh news, earnings, a strong sector theme, or an important technical breakout or breakdown level.

When to skip it

It's simple, but it's not something to trade on every chart.

Chop

Price keeps crossing above and below the 9 EMA, or the stock is mid-range on the daily chart. There's no clear momentum.

No momentum: nothing to hitchhike.

Not in play

Moving on weak volume, with no real catalyst and no real range.

Not in play: not this setup.

A late pullback

The third, fourth or fifth pullback after a move that's been going for hours. It may have exhausted itself.

Only the first clean one.

Heavy opposing volume

The pullback is aggressive, reclaims levels on strong volume, or the whole market is ripping against your short.

Heavy volume against you: pass.

Volume decides it

The initial move should happen on strong volume. The pullback into the 9 EMA should be lighter: on a long, the selling lighter than the buying was; on a short, the buying lighter than the selling was. Then, ideally, volume comes back as the trend resumes.

Same move, same 9 EMA. A stock pulling back toward the 9 EMA isn't automatically a trade. It's only an opportunity if the volume and the price action support it.

Check the pullback's volume

Average volume per 2-minute bar on the move, and on the pullback. The numbers start as the walkthrough's.

The move against the trend should look less convincing

A controlled pullback on light volume says the other side can't reverse the move. A pullback that's bouncing aggressively, reclaiming levels on strong volume, says it can.

Heavy volume against the trend is the warning not to take it.

The video doesn't put a number on "lighter". The 60% and 100% lines in the checker are ours, a starting point for your own review.

Size it off the 21 EMA

The 21 EMA is where the trade is wrong, so it's what you size from. Then the position splits: about 70% trimmed into momentum in small pieces, 30% left to trail. The default numbers are the walkthrough's, and the dollar risk is only an example.

Tighten it once it works

The 21 EMA close is the starting stop, not one you leave in place. Once there's a significant move or a new low on a short, tighten it to the most recent high. On a long, the most recent low.

Aggressive on profits: about 70% off into momentum.

Usually in smaller pieces, around 5% at a time, as it moves your way. The last 30% rides on the prior 2-minute bar, so you still participate if the move keeps going.

Stack it, or pass

The video closes with the list. The idea is to stack things in your favor. If they aren't all there, pass.

Check the market too

If you're looking to short a stock but the market is getting aggressively bought, that's something to consider. The same goes the other way for a long into a market that's being sold hard.

Prefer the first clean opportunity after the first strong move.

By then there's evidence of momentum, but the move isn't one that's already been going for hours and may have exhausted itself.

Don't reinvent it. Adapt it.

The trader in the video didn't invent the hitchhiker. It's a setup learned at SMB Capital, studied over time and adapted to the way they like to trade. That's how you build a playbook.

03

Write your rules

Here: the 2-minute chart, the 9 EMA pullback, the 21 EMA as the initial stop, and a specific way of taking profits into momentum.

Those rules make the trade repeatable, and that's the objective: something you can recognize, execute, manage and review the same way over and over again, not a completely different trade every time you sit down.

How you trade it

The whole setup, in order.

  1. Find a stock in play with real momentum: abnormal volume, a large range, and a reason.
  2. Let the outsized move go. If it's already extended, don't chase it.
  3. Wait for the first pullback toward the 9 EMA on the 2-minute chart, controlled and on lighter volume, ideally at another level too.
  4. Enter as the pullback slows and the original direction starts to resume, with volume coming back.
  5. Out on a 2-minute close past the 21 EMA. Size from it, and tighten to the most recent high or low once it works.
  6. Take about 70% into momentum in small pieces, and trail the last 30% on the prior 2-minute bar.

The indicator

One TradingView script for the setup (Pine Script v6). It finds the move, waits for the first pullback to the 9 EMA, checks the volume, and draws the entry, the stops, the trims and the runner's exit on your 2-minute chart. It marks and alerts. It does not place trades.

  • Hitchhiker Scalp

    alerts

    A move counts when price travels at least 2.5 ATRs in 6 bars, at least 4 of them in its direction, on 1.5× normal volume, and ends extended from the 9 EMA. It then waits for the first pullback to the 9 EMA that holds a close on the right side of the 21 EMA, and keeps the pullback's average volume as a percent of the move's. A bar that takes out the prior bar's high (or low, short) marks the entry a tick past it; if the pullback ran heavier than the move it marks “Skip” instead. The stop is a 2-minute close past the 21 EMA, tightened to the most recent low (high) once the move's extreme is taken out. Each new high in profit trims two 5% pieces up to 70%, then the last 30% is out on a break of the prior bar. Only the first pullback after a move is taken. Every number is a setting.

    Two things stay with you: whether the stock is really in play (news, earnings, a theme, a key level) and what the market is doing. The panel shows RVOL as a hint. The 60% and 100% volume lines are ours, not the video's.

//@version=6
// =====================================================================
// Hitchhiker Scalp — thetrademethod.com
//
// An SMB Capital trader's adapted hitchhiker scalp, on the 2-minute chart:
// don't chase the move, ride the first pullback to the 9 EMA.
//
//   1. The move      An outsized move: price travels at least N ATRs in a
//                    few bars, most of them in one direction, on volume
//                    well above normal, and ends extended from the 9 EMA.
//   2. The pullback  The FIRST pullback back toward the 9 EMA. It must
//                    hold a close on the right side of the 21 EMA.
//   3. The volume    The pullback's average volume against the move's.
//                    Lighter is the setup; heavier than the move = skip.
//   4. The entry     As the pullback slows: a bar takes out the prior
//                    bar's high (long) or low (short) near the 9 EMA.
//                    Entry is marked a tick past that prior bar.
//   5. The stop      A 2-minute CLOSE past the 21 EMA. Once the trade
//                    makes a new high (low) past the move's, the stop
//                    tightens to the most recent low (high).
//   6. The exit      About 70% trimmed into momentum, ~5% at a time, on
//                    bars that push to new highs (lows). The last 30% is
//                    trailed on the prior bar: out when a bar breaks the
//                    prior bar's low (long) or high (short).
//
// "In play" (news, earnings, a theme, a key level) and the market's
// direction are still your call; the panel shows RVOL as a hint.
// Marks and alerts only; it places no orders.
// =====================================================================
indicator("Hitchhiker Scalp", shorttitle = "Hitchhiker", overlay = true, max_labels_count = 300, max_lines_count = 100)

// ---------------- inputs ----------------
grpS = "Setup"
sideIn     = input.string("Both", "Trade", options = ["Long", "Short", "Both"], group = grpS)
fastLen    = input.int(9, "Entry EMA (the 9)", minval = 2, group = grpS)
slowLen    = input.int(21, "Stop EMA (the 21)", minval = 3, group = grpS)
atrLen     = input.int(14, "ATR length", minval = 2, group = grpS)

grpM = "The move"
moveBars   = input.int(6, "Bars in the move", minval = 3, maxval = 20, group = grpM)
moveAtr    = input.float(2.5, "Move size, in ATRs", minval = 0.5, step = 0.1, group = grpM)
moveVolX   = input.float(1.5, "Move volume vs normal (x)", minval = 1.0, step = 0.1, group = grpM)
volLen     = input.int(50, "Normal volume: average of", minval = 5, group = grpM)
extAtr     = input.float(0.8, "Extended from the 9 EMA, in ATRs", minval = 0.0, step = 0.1, group = grpM)

grpP = "The pullback"
touchAtr   = input.float(0.25, "Counts as at the 9 EMA within (ATRs)", minval = 0.0, step = 0.05, group = grpP)
maxPbBars  = input.int(12, "Give up after this many pullback bars", minval = 2, group = grpP)
lightPct   = input.float(60, "Light: pullback volume at most (% of move)", minval = 1, group = grpP)
heavyPct   = input.float(100, "Skip: pullback volume above (% of move)", minval = 1, group = grpP)

grpX = "Management"
trimTotal  = input.float(70, "Trim into momentum (%)", minval = 0, maxval = 100, group = grpX)
trimPiece  = input.float(5, "Each piece (%)", minval = 1, maxval = 100, group = grpX)
piecesBar  = input.int(2, "Pieces per new-high bar", minval = 1, group = grpX)
tighten    = input.bool(true, "Tighten to the most recent low/high on a new extreme", group = grpX)

grpV = "Display"
showEma    = input.bool(true, "Show the 9 and 21 EMAs", group = grpV)
showTrims  = input.bool(true, "Mark each trim", group = grpV)
showPanel  = input.bool(true, "Show the panel", group = grpV)

allowLong  = sideIn != "Short"
allowShort = sideIn != "Long"
tick       = syminfo.mintick

// ---------------- series ----------------
e9      = ta.ema(close, fastLen)
e21     = ta.ema(close, slowLen)
atr     = ta.atr(atrLen)
volNorm = ta.sma(volume, volLen)
rvol    = volNorm[1] > 0 ? volume / volNorm[1] : na

plot(showEma ? e9 : na, "9 EMA", color.new(#ff9f0a, 0), 2)
plot(showEma ? e21 : na, "21 EMA", color.new(#bf5af2, 0), 2)

// the move: net travel, bars in the direction, volume, extension
net     = close - close[moveBars]
upBars  = math.sum(close > open ? 1 : 0, moveBars)
dnBars  = math.sum(close < open ? 1 : 0, moveBars)
mVol    = ta.sma(volume, moveBars)
mNorm   = volNorm[moveBars]
volOk   = mNorm > 0 and mVol >= moveVolX * mNorm
moveUp   = allowLong  and net >=  moveAtr * atr and upBars >= moveBars - 2 and volOk and close - e9 >= extAtr * atr
moveDown = allowShort and net <= -moveAtr * atr and dnBars >= moveBars - 2 and volOk and e9 - close >= extAtr * atr
hhMove  = ta.highest(high, moveBars)
llMove  = ta.lowest(low, moveBars)

// ---------------- state ----------------
// 0 idle · 1 move in, waiting for the pullback · 2 at the 9 EMA, waiting for the turn · 3 in the trade
var int   state    = 0
var int   dir      = 0
var float moveExt  = na     // the move's high (long) or low (short)
var float moveVAvg = na
var int   extBar   = na
var float pbVSum   = 0.0
var int   pbN      = 0
var float entry    = na
var float risk     = na
var float tight    = na
var float best     = na
var float trimmed  = 0.0
var float trimSum  = 0.0    // sum of (piece % x price), for the average
var bool  runner   = false
var float lastR    = na
var int   lastEnd  = na     // bar where the last setup ended: only a fresh move counts after it

float pbPct = pbN > 0 and moveVAvg > 0 ? pbVSum / pbN / moveVAvg * 100 : na

// alert flags for this bar
bool aMove  = false
bool aTouch = false
bool aEntry = false
bool aSkip  = false
bool aTight = false
bool aTrim  = false
bool aExit  = false

// a new move: starts (or flips) the hunt for the first pullback. After a setup ends,
// only a move that started after it counts, so the 2nd, 3rd... pullback is never taken.
int  mdir     = moveUp ? 1 : moveDown ? -1 : 0
bool fresh    = na(lastEnd) or bar_index - moveBars >= lastEnd
bool startNew = state <= 2 and mdir != 0 and fresh and (state == 0 or mdir != dir)

if startNew
    state    := 1
    dir      := mdir
    moveExt  := mdir == 1 ? hhMove : llMove
    moveVAvg := mVol
    extBar   := bar_index
    pbVSum   := 0.0
    pbN      := 0
    aMove    := true
    label.new(bar_index, mdir == 1 ? high : low, "Move · don't chase", style = mdir == 1 ? label.style_label_down : label.style_label_up,
              color = color.new(color.gray, 70), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
else if state == 1 or state == 2
    // the move extends: new extreme before any pullback touch
    bool newExt = dir == 1 ? high > moveExt : low < moveExt
    if state == 1 and newExt
        moveExt := dir == 1 ? high : low
        extBar  := bar_index
        moveVAvg := (moveVAvg * 3 + volume) / 4
        pbVSum  := 0.0
        pbN     := 0
    else
        pbVSum += volume
        pbN    += 1
    pbPct := pbN > 0 and moveVAvg > 0 ? pbVSum / pbN / moveVAvg * 100 : na
    bool atNine  = dir == 1 ? low <= e9 + touchAtr * atr : high >= e9 - touchAtr * atr
    bool tooDeep = dir == 1 ? close < e21 : close > e21
    if tooDeep
        label.new(bar_index, dir == 1 ? low : high, "Through the 21 · no trade", style = dir == 1 ? label.style_label_up : label.style_label_down,
                  color = color.new(color.gray, 70), textcolor = color.white, size = size.tiny)
        state   := 0
        lastEnd := bar_index
    else if pbN > maxPbBars
        state   := 0
        lastEnd := bar_index
    else if state == 1 and atNine and pbN > 0
        state  := 2
        aTouch := true
    if state == 2
        bool turn = dir == 1 ? high > high[1] and close > e9 - touchAtr * atr : low < low[1] and close < e9 + touchAtr * atr
        if turn
            if not na(pbPct) and pbPct > heavyPct
                label.new(bar_index, dir == 1 ? low : high, "Skip · heavy volume " + str.tostring(pbPct, "#") + "%",
                          style = dir == 1 ? label.style_label_up : label.style_label_down, color = color.new(#ff453a, 30), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
                aSkip   := true
                state   := 0
                lastEnd := bar_index
            else
                entry   := dir == 1 ? high[1] + tick : low[1] - tick
                risk    := math.abs(entry - e21)
                tight   := na
                best    := dir == 1 ? high : low
                trimmed := 0.0
                trimSum := 0.0
                runner  := false
                state   := 3
                aEntry  := true
                string grade = na(pbPct) ? "" : pbPct <= lightPct ? " · light vol " : " · vol "
                label.new(bar_index, dir == 1 ? low : high, (dir == 1 ? "Buy " : "Short ") + str.tostring(entry, format.mintick) + grade +
                          (na(pbPct) ? "" : str.tostring(pbPct, "#") + "%"), style = dir == 1 ? label.style_label_up : label.style_label_down,
                          color = dir == 1 ? color.new(#30d158, 0) : color.new(#ff453a, 0), textcolor = color.black, size = size.small)
else if state == 3
    float exitPx = na
    string why = ""
    // 1. the stop: a close past the 21 EMA
    if dir == 1 ? close < e21 : close > e21
        exitPx := close
        why    := "Close past the 21"
    // 2. the tightened stop
    if na(exitPx) and not na(tight) and (dir == 1 ? low <= tight : high >= tight)
        exitPx := dir == 1 ? math.min(open, tight) : math.max(open, tight)
        why    := "Tight stop"
    // 3. the runner: a break of the prior bar
    if na(exitPx) and runner and (dir == 1 ? low < low[1] : high > high[1])
        exitPx := dir == 1 ? math.min(open, low[1] - tick) : math.max(open, high[1] + tick)
        why    := "Prior bar broken"
    if na(exitPx)
        bool newBest = dir == 1 ? high > best : low < best
        if newBest
            best := dir == 1 ? high : low
            // tighten once price takes out the move's extreme
            if tighten and (dir == 1 ? high > moveExt : low < moveExt)
                float t = dir == 1 ? low[1] - tick : high[1] + tick
                if na(tight) or (dir == 1 ? t > tight : t < tight)
                    tight  := t
                    aTight := true
            // trim into momentum, only in profit
            if trimmed < trimTotal and (dir == 1 ? close > entry : close < entry)
                float add = math.min(trimTotal - trimmed, trimPiece * piecesBar)
                trimmed += add
                trimSum += add * close
                aTrim := true
                if showTrims
                    label.new(bar_index, dir == 1 ? high : low, "", style = dir == 1 ? label.style_triangledown : label.style_triangleup,
                              yloc = dir == 1 ? yloc.abovebar : yloc.belowbar, color = color.new(#ff453a, 0), size = size.tiny)
                if trimmed >= trimTotal
                    runner := true
    if not na(exitPx)
        float rest  = 100 - trimmed
        float avgPx = (trimSum + rest * exitPx) / 100
        lastR := risk > 0 ? dir * (avgPx - entry) / risk : na
        label.new(bar_index, dir == 1 ? high : low, why + "\n" + (lastR >= 0 ? "+" : "") + str.tostring(lastR, "#.0") + "R",
                  style = dir == 1 ? label.style_label_down : label.style_label_up, color = color.new(#22d3ee, 0), textcolor = color.black, size = size.small)
        aExit   := true
        state   := 0
        lastEnd := bar_index

// the stop lines while in the trade
plot(state == 3 ? e21 : na, "Stop: close past the 21", color.new(#bf5af2, 0), 1, plot.style_linebr)
plot(state == 3 ? tight : na, "Tight stop", color.new(#ff453a, 0), 2, plot.style_linebr)
plot(state == 3 and runner ? (dir == 1 ? low[1] - tick : high[1] + tick) : na, "Runner trail", color.new(#22d3ee, 0), 1, plot.style_stepline)
bgcolor(state == 2 ? color.new(#ff9f0a, 92) : na, title = "At the 9 EMA")

// ---------------- panel ----------------
var table panel = table.new(position.top_right, 2, 7, bgcolor = color.new(#1d1d1f, 5), border_color = color.new(#3a3a3c, 0), border_width = 1)
if showPanel and barstate.islast
    string st = state == 0 ? "Waiting for a move" : state == 1 ? "Move in · wait for the 9" : state == 2 ? "At the 9 · wait for the turn" : (dir == 1 ? "Long" : "Short") + " · " + str.tostring(trimmed, "#") + "% trimmed"
    color  txt = color.new(#f5f5f7, 0)
    table.cell(panel, 0, 0, "Hitchhiker", text_color = color.new(#ff9f0a, 0), text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(panel, 1, 0, st, text_color = txt, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(panel, 0, 1, "RVOL (bar)", text_color = txt, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(panel, 1, 1, na(rvol) ? "—" : str.tostring(rvol, "#.0") + "x", text_color = txt, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(panel, 0, 2, "Pullback vol", text_color = txt, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(panel, 1, 2, state == 1 or state == 2 ? (na(pbPct) ? "—" : str.tostring(pbPct, "#") + "% of move") : "—",
               text_color = state >= 1 and state <= 2 and not na(pbPct) ? (pbPct <= lightPct ? color.new(#30d158, 0) : pbPct > heavyPct ? color.new(#ff453a, 0) : color.new(#f4b942, 0)) : txt, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(panel, 0, 3, "Entry / risk", text_color = txt, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(panel, 1, 3, state == 3 ? str.tostring(entry, format.mintick) + " / " + str.tostring(risk, format.mintick) : "—", text_color = txt, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(panel, 0, 4, "Stop", text_color = txt, text_size = size.small)

Being right about direction isn't enough.

A good trade isn't just about where a stock is going. It's about where you get in, where you're wrong, and how you manage it. You don't need to catch the beginning of every move. If a stock is already extended, let it go, wait for the first pullback, and watch how price and volume behave.
