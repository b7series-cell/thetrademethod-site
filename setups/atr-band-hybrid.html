What is the ATR Hybrid Scalp?

The ATR Hybrid Scalp is a 5-minute Micro Nasdaq futures (MNQ) system where longs and shorts are different strategies. Longs buy a touch of a band 3.1 times the ATR below the previous close, with a 2 ATR target and a 1.5 ATR stop. Shorts sell after a candle with a long upper wick and exit at the close of the next candle.

1

Understand ATR — the foundation this whole system is built on

  • ATR (Average True Range) is a measure of volatility — roughly, how much the market has typically been moving per candle recently. It does not care about direction; a big move up and a big move down both push it higher.
  • A 50-point move means something completely different in a compressed, low-ATR market than it does in a highly volatile one. ATR gives you that context.

Every other number in this system — the entry band, the target, the stop — is a multiple of ATR. If you don't understand what it's actually measuring, the rest is just arbitrary math.

2

Anchor the bands to the previous candle's close

  • The entry band is an envelope around price, set by taking the current ATR reading and multiplying it by 3.1 — wider than a plain 1× ATR band, which means fewer touches, but more significant ones.
  • Critically, that distance is always measured from the previous candle's closing price, not a constantly-moving live price. Without a static anchor, the bands would just run away from price forever and never get touched.

A band that updates every tick off the live price can never be caught. Anchoring it to a fixed, already-closed reference point is what makes the band an actual level instead of a moving target.

3

Longs: enter the instant the lower band is touched

  • There is no additional filter. Whenever a candle's lower wick clips the lower ATR band, that is a long entry — on this system, the band is only ever used for longs, never shorts.
  • It's placed as a limit order that dynamically updates every 5 minutes as the band's anchor point shifts, rather than chasing price with a market order.

It sounds almost too simple to be a complete entry model on its own — but the band's width (3.1× ATR) is already doing the filtering. A touch that far out is a genuinely stretched move, not noise.

4

Longs: a fixed 2 / 1.5 ATR exit, decided before the trade

  • Target: 2× the previous candle's ATR reading, added to the entry. Stop: 1.5× the same ATR reading, subtracted from the entry. Both are fixed the moment the trade is taken and don't move afterward.
  • The trader calls this one extremely important: never trade the opening candle. Volatility right at the open is too unpredictable for the band and the ATR reading behind it to be trusted yet. (He calls the 3.1 multiple crucial too.)

Because both the target and the stop scale with current volatility, the trade's risk and reward automatically resize themselves to the market's actual conditions instead of using a fixed dollar or point value.

5

Shorts: a completely different trigger

  • No band, no ATR multiple. The short side looks for one candle shape: a long upper wick — a rejection off the highs, whether the candle itself closes green or red. The trader judges that wick by eye. To test it, the video's presenter made it exact: an upper wick at least 2× the body, and a lower wick no bigger than about a tenth of the upper wick.
  • Entered at the market, generally at the open of the very next candle after the pattern completes — no order flow, no secondary indicator, no confirmation layer beyond the candle itself.

Inverting the long model for shorts was tested directly and simply didn't work. This is why: shorts here run on price-action structure, not on a volatility band, because the two directions of this market don't behave symmetrically.

6

Shorts: exit on time, not on price

  • There is no profit target and no stop-loss on this side. The trade is closed no matter what at the close of the very next candle after entry — a single 5-minute hold, full stop.
  • The presenter tried one extra filter: only short after the prior day's candle closed red. It looked better, but on only about 60 trades, too few to trust. It's the presenter's idea, not the trader's.

A time-based exit only makes sense because the entry is a specific, short-lived structural read, not a directional thesis meant to play out over hours. Once that one candle is over, the edge the pattern gave you is already spent.

Why the two sides don't share a rulebook

Same chart, same trader, but the long and short models are built from entirely different ideas.

ATR measures magnitude, not direction

A band built from ATR treats up-moves and down-moves identically. On this system it's only ever used to time longs — inverting it for shorts was tested and simply broke.

The anchor has to be static

Bands measured from the previous candle's close, not the live price, are what makes them a fixed level worth watching instead of a moving target that's never touched.

Position size follows ATR, not the other way around

As ATR (and the stop distance built from it) grows, contract size has to shrink to keep the dollar risk on each trade roughly constant.

The four filters

These don't create either entry — the band touch and the wick pattern do that. They keep you off the versions of this system that quietly stop working.

1  Trading the opening candle

Volatility right at the open is too unpredictable for the ATR reading behind the band, or the target and stop built from it, to be trusted yet. The video gives this rule for the longs. We skip the opening candle for shorts too.

Kills: a band touch or wick pattern that formed on unreliable data.

2  Inverting the long model for shorts

Tested directly: simply flipping the ATR band logic to short the upper band instead of buying the lower one does not work on this market.

Kills: treating two genuinely different strategies as one symmetric rule.

3  Fixed contract size regardless of ATR

The same number of contracts on a wide-ATR day risks meaningfully more than on a narrow one, because the stop distance itself is built from ATR.

Kills: a volatility expansion silently doubling your risk per trade.

4  Every upper wick, regardless of daily trend

In the presenter's test the short pattern did better when the prior day's candle closed red, but on only about 60 trades. Treat it as a lead to test, not a proven rule. It's the presenter's idea, not the trader's.

Kills: a valid-looking wick taken in the wrong broader context.

The mistake this prevents

Trade a fixed number of contracts regardless of how wide ATR makes the stop. Five MNQ contracts (worth $10 per point each) risk roughly $400 on a 40-point stop.

The same five contracts risk roughly $800 — double — on an 80-point stop, with no change in contract count at all.

Position size has to shrink as ATR, and the stop distance built from it, grows. Otherwise a volatility expansion doubles your risk without you doing anything differently.

What "hybrid scalping" actually means here

The two sides don't just use different triggers — they're managed on entirely different logic once you're in.

The long side is managed by price: a fixed target and stop, both scaled to volatility, and the trade stays open until one of them is hit — however long that takes. The short side is managed by time: there's no target or stop at all, just a single candle's worth of exposure before the trade is closed regardless of where price is. One side asks "how far will this move," the other asks "what happens in exactly the next five minutes." Running both off the same chart, with the same instrument, is what makes this a hybrid rather than a single repeatable formula applied to both directions.

//@version=6
// =====================================================================
// ATR Hybrid Scalp — thetrademethod.com
//
// Two playbooks on one chart, from a prop-firm trader's MNQ account as
// rebuilt in an IQCapital video. Made for a 5-minute chart (MNQ or any
// US futures; it works on stocks and ETFs too).
//
//   LONGS (the trader's model)
//   1. Band      For every candle: the PREVIOUS close minus 3.1 x the
//                previous candle's ATR(14). Fixed before the candle opens.
//   2. Entry     A limit buy at the band. Any candle whose low reaches it
//                is a buy (at the band, or at the open if it opens below).
//                No other filter.
//   3. Exits     Target = entry + 2 x that same ATR, stop = entry - 1.5 x
//                it, rounded to the tick and fixed at entry. Stop first,
//                gaps fill at the open. On the entry candle only the stop,
//                plus the target if the candle CLOSES at or past it.
//   4. Never trade the opening candle (the trader's "extremely
//                important" rule).
//
//   SHORTS (a different trigger, not the band)
//   5. Wick      A candle (green or red) with an upper wick at least 2x its
//                body and a lower wick no more than 1/10 of the upper wick.
//                The trader judges the wick by eye; these numbers are the
//                video presenter's test version.
//   6. Trade     Short the next candle's open, cover at that candle's
//                close. No target, no stop.
//   7. Filter    Prior regular session closed red (its last close under its
//                first open). The presenter's test, on only about 60
//                trades, so it can be switched off.
//
// Use a chart with regular trading hours only (on futures, set the chart's
// session to regular), so ATR and the band come from regular-session
// candles like the page's chart. On a 24-hour chart the overnight candles
// feed ATR too, though trades are still only marked inside the session.
//
// Ours: skipping the opening candle for shorts too, the 9:30-16:00 New
// York session used for "opening candle" and "prior day", and one trade
// at a time. Same rules as the page's example chart (js/atr-live.js).
// Signals use closed candles only. Draws and alerts; it places no orders.
// =====================================================================
indicator("ATR Hybrid Scalp", shorttitle = "ATR Hybrid", overlay = true, max_labels_count = 500, max_lines_count = 500)

// ---------------- inputs ----------------
atrLen    = input.int(14, "ATR length", minval = 1, group = "Longs")
kMult     = input.float(3.1, "Band = previous close − ATR ×", minval = 0.1, step = 0.1, group = "Longs")
tgtMult   = input.float(2.0, "Target (× ATR)", minval = 0.1, step = 0.1, group = "Longs")
stpMult   = input.float(1.5, "Stop (× ATR)", minval = 0.1, step = 0.1, group = "Longs")
doLongs   = input.bool(true, "Mark longs", group = "Longs")
doShorts  = input.bool(true, "Mark shorts", group = "Shorts")
wickX     = input.float(2.0, "Upper wick ≥ body ×", minval = 0.1, step = 0.1, group = "Shorts")
lowFrac   = input.float(0.1, "Lower wick ≤ upper wick ×", minval = 0.0, step = 0.05, group = "Shorts")
needRed   = input.bool(true, "Only after a red prior day (presenter's test)", group = "Shorts")
skipOpenS = input.bool(true, "Skip the opening candle for shorts too (ours)", group = "Shorts")
sess      = input.session("0930-1600", "Regular session (New York)", group = "Session")
riskUsd   = input.float(500.0, "Risk per trade ($) for contract size", minval = 0.0, group = "Display")
showBand  = input.bool(true, "Draw the band", group = "Display")
showPanel = input.bool(true, "Show status panel", group = "Display")

// ---------------- series ----------------
tz     = "America/New_York"
atr    = ta.atr(atrLen)                      // Wilder (RMA), like the page
tick   = syminfo.mintick
band   = na(atr[1]) ? na : math.floor((close[1] - kMult * atr[1]) / tick + 1e-7) * tick
inSess = not na(time(timeframe.period, sess, tz))
newDay = dayofmonth(time, tz) != dayofmonth(time[1], tz)
openBar = inSess and (not inSess[1] or newDay)
nextT  = time + timeframe.in_seconds() * 1000
endMs  = timestamp(tz, year(time, tz), month(time, tz), dayofmonth(time, tz), 16, 0)
lastBar = inSess and nextT >= endMs
rnd(x) => math.round(x / tick) * tick

plot(showBand ? band : na, "Band (prev close − 3.1 ATR)", color = color.new(#2997ff, 0), linewidth = 2, style = plot.style_stepline)

// ---------------- prior regular session: red or green ----------------
var float sO = na
var float sC = na
var float pO = na
var float pC = na
var bool  pFull = false
var bool  sFull = false
if openBar
    pO := sO, pC := sC, pFull := sFull
    sO := open, sFull := false
if inSess
    sC := close
    sFull := true                     // the session traded (half days count too)
priorRed = pFull and not na(pO) and pC < pO
priorTxt = not pFull or na(pO) ? "unknown" : pC < pO ? "red" : pC > pO ? "green" : "flat"

// ---------------- state ----------------
var int   lPos  = 0
var int   lBar  = na
var float lE    = na
var float lT    = na
var float lS    = na
var line  eLn   = na
var line  tLn   = na
var line  sLn   = na
var bool  sNext = false            // a short is on for the candle after the signal
bool evBuy = false, evTgt = false, evStop = false, evWick = false, evShortOut = false
string skipWhy = ""

body  = math.abs(close - open)
upW   = high - math.max(open, close)
loW   = math.min(open, close) - low
isWick = high > low and upW >= wickX * body and loW <= lowFrac * upW
touch = not na(band) and low <= band

// ---------------- 1. a short from the last candle: in at this open, out at this close ----------------
busy = false
if barstate.isconfirmed and sNext
    sNext := false
    busy := true
    evShortOut := true
    pts = open - close
    label.new(bar_index, high, "Short " + str.tostring(open, format.mintick) + " → " + str.tostring(close, format.mintick) + "\n" + (pts >= 0 ? "+" : "") + str.tostring(pts, format.mintick) + " pts",
         style = label.style_label_down, color = color.new(pts >= 0 ? #30d158 : #ff453a, 15), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)

// ---------------- 2. the open long ----------------
if barstate.isconfirmed and lPos == 1 and not busy
    float x = na
    string why = ""
    if bar_index > lBar and open <= lS
        x := open, why := "stop"
    else if low <= lS
        x := lS, why := "stop"
    else if bar_index > lBar and open >= lT
        x := open, why := "target"
    else if bar_index > lBar and high >= lT
        x := lT, why := "target"
    if why != ""
        r = (x - lE) / (lE - lS)
        evTgt := why == "target"
        evStop := why == "stop"
        label.new(bar_index, why == "target" ? high : low, (why == "target" ? "Target " : "Stop ") + str.tostring(x, format.mintick) + "\n" + (r >= 0 ? "+" : "") + str.tostring(r, "0.00") + "R",
             style = why == "target" ? label.style_label_down : label.style_label_up, color = color.new(why == "target" ? #30d158 : #ff453a, 15), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
        line.set_x2(eLn, bar_index), line.set_x2(tLn, bar_index), line.set_x2(sLn, bar_index)
        lPos := 0
    else
        line.set_x2(eLn, bar_index + 1), line.set_x2(tLn, bar_index + 1), line.set_x2(sLn, bar_index + 1)
    busy := true                       // in a trade, or out on this very candle: no new entries on it

// ---------------- 3. new entries (flat, closed candle) ----------------
if barstate.isconfirmed and not busy
    tookLong = false
    if doLongs and touch and inSess
        if openBar
            skipWhy := "open"
            label.new(bar_index, low, "open · skip", style = label.style_label_up, color = color.new(#636366, 30), textcolor = color.white, size = size.tiny)
        else
            A = atr[1]
            lE := math.min(open, band)
            lT := rnd(lE + tgtMult * A)
            lS := rnd(lE - stpMult * A)
            lBar := bar_index, lPos := 1, tookLong := true
            evBuy := true
            risk = lE - lS
            n = risk > 0 and syminfo.pointvalue > 0 ? math.floor(riskUsd / (risk * syminfo.pointvalue)) : 0
            label.new(bar_index, low, "Buy " + str.tostring(lE, format.mintick) + "\ntarget " + str.tostring(lT, format.mintick) + " · stop " + str.tostring(lS, format.mintick) + (riskUsd > 0 ? "\n" + str.tostring(n) + (syminfo.type == "futures" ? " contracts" : " sh") : ""),
                 style = label.style_label_up, color = color.new(#2997ff, 10), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
            eLn := line.new(bar_index, lE, bar_index + 1, lE, color = #2997ff, width = 2)
            tLn := line.new(bar_index, lT, bar_index + 1, lT, color = #30d158, style = line.style_dashed)
            sLn := line.new(bar_index, lS, bar_index + 1, lS, color = #ff453a, style = line.style_dashed)
            // the entry candle: the stop, or the target if it closed at or past it
            if low <= lS
                label.new(bar_index, low, "Stop on the entry candle\n-1.00R", style = label.style_label_up, color = color.new(#ff453a, 15), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
                evStop := true, lPos := 0
            else if close >= lT
                label.new(bar_index, high, "Target on the entry candle", style = label.style_label_down, color = color.new(#30d158, 15), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)
                evTgt := true, lPos := 0
    // the wick test doesn't apply on a long's entry candle
    if doShorts and not tookLong and isWick and inSess
        if openBar and skipOpenS
            skipWhy := "open"
        else if needRed and not priorRed
            skipWhy := "prior day " + priorTxt
        else if lastBar
            skipWhy := "last candle"
        else
            sNext := true
            evWick := true
            label.new(bar_index, high, "Wick · short the next open", style = label.style_label_down, color = color.new(#ff9f0a, 10), textcolor = color.white, size = size.small)

plotshape(doShorts and barstate.isconfirmed and isWick and inSess and not evWick and skipWhy != "", "Wick skipped", shape.xcross, location.abovebar, color.new(#8e8e93, 0), size = size.tiny)

// ---------------- status panel ----------------
var table pnl = table.new(position.top_right, 1, 3, bgcolor = color.new(#1c1c1e, 10))
if showPanel and barstate.islast
    st = lPos == 1 ? "Long " + str.tostring(lE, format.mintick) + " · target " + str.tostring(lT, format.mintick) + " · stop " + str.tostring(lS, format.mintick) :
         sNext ? "Short at the next open, out at its close" : "Flat · limit buy at the next band"
    nb = close - kMult * atr
    table.cell(pnl, 0, 0, "ATR Hybrid Scalp", text_color = color.new(#f5f5f7, 0), text_size = size.small, text_halign = text.align_left)
    table.cell(pnl, 0, 1, st, text_color = color.new(#a1a1a6, 0), text_size = size.small, text_halign = text.align_left)
    table.cell(pnl, 0, 2, "ATR " + str.tostring(atr, "0.00") + " · next band " + str.tostring(nb, format.mintick) + " · prior day " + priorTxt, text_color = color.new(#a1a1a6, 0), text_size = size.small, text_halign = text.align_left)

// ---------------- alerts ----------------
alertcondition(evBuy, "Long: band touched", "ATR Hybrid: {{ticker}} touched the band. Long with a 2 ATR target and 1.5 ATR stop.")
alertcondition(evTgt or evStop, "Long: target or stop", "ATR Hybrid: {{ticker}} long hit its target or stop.")
alertcondition(evWick, "Short: rejection wick", "ATR Hybrid: {{ticker}} rejection wick. Short the next open, cover at its close.")

Questions

Why is the band anchored to the previous close?

A band that moves with live price can never be caught. Measuring from the last closed candle makes it a fixed level for the next 5 minutes.

What is the short signal?

A candle with a long upper wick, a rejection off the highs. The presenter's test version needs an upper wick at least twice the body and a lower wick no more than about a tenth of the upper wick. Enter at the next candle's open and close at that candle's close, with no target or stop.

Why don't the shorts mirror the longs?

Inverting the long model for shorts was tested in the source and didn't work. In the presenter's test, shorts did better after a red prior day, but on only about 60 trades.

Does the ATR Hybrid Scalp work?

Not on our stand-in test: with the page's indicator rules on QQQ and SPY 5-minute bars, 2,108 trades had a 43% win rate, a 0.87 profit factor and -0.04R per trade. The longs were about break-even; the shorts lost. A test on MNQ futures could come out differently.

Same market. Two different games.

The long side reads volatility with an ATR band and manages risk with fixed multiples of it. The short side reads a single candle shape and manages risk with a stopwatch. Both can be real edges — but only when each is traded on its own terms.

  • Never trade the opening candle on the long side — the ATR reading behind the band isn't reliable yet.
  • Don't invert the long model for shorts. Tested head-to-head, that simply falls apart.
  • Size off the current ATR, not a fixed contract count, or a volatility expansion doubles your risk without you doing anything differently.
